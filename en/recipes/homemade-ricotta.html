Professional Homemade Ricotta

Just milk, lemon juice, and salt — elevated into pillowy restaurant curds purely through exact temperature control (180°F / 83°C held for 40 minutes) and suspended gravity draining.

Milk
½ Gallon (~1.75+ Liters)
Target Temp
Exact 180°F (83°C)
Curd Hold
Exact 40 Minutes

Total Time
~2 Hours
Steps 1–3 · 180°F Coagulation Step 4 · Initial Drain Step 5 · Suspended Hang Step 6 · Season

Ingredients & Gear

Accuracy lives entirely in the instant-read thermometer and timer.

Ingredients
Whole Milk½ gallon (~1.75+ L)
Lemon Juice¼ cup (60 ml)
SaltTo taste (at the very end)
 Equipment
Medium saucepan, Instant-read thermometer, Fine-mesh sieve, Cheesecloth, Kitchen twine, Ladle & Large bowlsFull set
Steps 1–3

Slow Heat to 180°F & 40-Minute Hold

  1. Heat Milk

    Slowly warm milk in a medium saucepan while monitoring closely with an instant-read thermometer until it reaches an exact 180°F (83°C).

  2. Stir in Lemon & Hold 40 Minutes

    Gently stir in ¼ cup (60 ml) lemon juice and maintain heat right at 180°F (83°C) for an exact 40 minutes, stirring occasionally until curds separate cleanly from translucent whey.

Step 4

5-Minute Sieve Drain

  1. Turn off heat and cool briefly. Ladle curds gently into a cheesecloth-lined fine sieve set over a large bowl and let drain naturally for 5 minutes.

Step 5

1 to 1.5-Hour Cabinet Handle Hang

  1. Gather and twist cheesecloth corners, tie tight with kitchen twine leaving long tails, suspend from a cabinet handle over a bowl, and let drip undisturbed for 1 to 1.5 hours.

Step 6

Break Curd & Season

  1. Unwrap into a bowl, poke a hole in the center to break up the curd block, season with a pinch of salt to taste, and fold gently until creamy. Ready directly for Detroit-Style Pan Pizza or Whipped Lemon Ricotta!
