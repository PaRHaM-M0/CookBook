Lacto-Fermented Spicy Tomato Sauce

Professional Probiotic Pizza Sauce — quartered heirloom tomatoes, halved long red chilies, and whole garlic cloves submerged in an exact 2% salt brine for 120 hours, then emulsified smooth with olive oil and tomato paste.

Exact Brine
1,000 g Water + 20 g Kosher Salt
Room-Temp Ferment
Exact 5 Full Days (120h)
Vessel
3-Quart Deli Container / Jar
Finish
High-Speed Raw Emulsion
Skill Level
Advanced
Phase 1 · 2% Brine & 5-Day Ferment Phase 2 · Blend & Finish

Ingredients & Gear

Digital gram scale mandatory for exact 2% brine salinity.

For the Ferment
Water1,000 g
Kosher Salt20 g
Heirloom Tomatoes, quartered5 whole
Long Red Chilies (Red Jalapeños / Fresno), stemmed & halved5 chilies
Whole Garlic cloves, peeled3 cloves
 For Blending & Gear
Extra-Virgin Olive Oil + Tomato Paste + Salt if needed2 tbsp (25 g) + 1 tbsp
3-quart container, Scale, Whisk, Plastic wrap + Water weight, Cheesecloth, Twine, Slotted spoon & High-speed blenderFull set
Phase 1

Brine & Submerged 120-Hour Ferment

  1. Dissolve 2% Brine

    Zero your 3-quart container on a scale, pour in 1,000 g water and 20 g kosher salt, and whisk vigorously until dissolved.

  2. Submerge Produce Completely (Crucial!)

    Drop in quartered heirloom tomatoes, halved red chilies, and peeled garlic. Press plastic wrap flush against the liquid surface and place a small water-filled container on top so 100% of solids stay submerged beneath the brine.

  3. Drape, Date & Ferment 5 Full Days

    Tie cheesecloth taut around the rim with twine so gas vents out while dust stays out, tape today's date onto the side, and rest at room temperature for 5 full days (120 hours).

Phase 2

High-Speed Emulsion (Solids Only!)

  1. Fish all fermented solids out with a slotted spoon straight into a high-speed blender — do NOT pour the leftover brine liquid in.

  2. Add 2 tbsp (25 g) extra-virgin olive oil and 1 tbsp tomato paste, blend on high until velvety and emulsified, adjust salt if needed, and blast briefly under high oven heat atop our Neapolitan Pizza!
