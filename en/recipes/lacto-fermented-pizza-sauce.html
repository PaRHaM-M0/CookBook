Lacto-Fermented Spicy Tomato Sauce

Pro Lacto-Fermented Spicy Tomato Sauce — documented step-for-step from the reference video. Quartered heirloom tomatoes, red chilies, and garlic cloves ferment submerged in a precision salt brine for strictly 5 full days (120 hours), then get lifted free of the brine and high-speed emulsified with extra-virgin olive oil and tomato paste. Chef's upfront note: this sauce packs real heat.

2% Brine
1000 g water + 20 g Kosher Salt
Fermentation
Strictly 5 full days (120h), room temp
Vessel
3-quart container (deli tub or large jar)
Finish
High-speed blender until silky & emulsified
Skill Level
Advanced
Phase 1: 2% Brine & Submersion Phase 2: Draining & High-Speed Emulsion

Ingredients & Equipment

A digital gram scale is mandatory — lactic fermentation safety and flavor depend on exact percentages.

Salt (only at the very end if needed)To taste

Phase 1

Building the 2% Brine & Submerging Produce

Ambient fermentation: strictly 5 full days (120 hours) — tape start date on container

  1. 1. Mix the Exact 2% Salt Brine

    Set your empty 3-quart container on the digital scale and tare. Pour in 1000 g water followed by exactly 20 g Kosher Salt, whisking vigorously until every crystal dissolves completely.

  2. 2. Submerge the Produce

    Drop the quartered heirloom tomatoes, halved red chilies, and whole peeled garlic cloves into the brine.

  3. 3. Lock Everything Beneath the Surface (Critical Step)

    Press a sheet of plastic wrap directly down onto the liquid surface and set a light weight on top (such as a smaller deli cup holding water). Keeping 100% of solid produce submerged anaerobically under brine prevents surface mold and spoilage.

  4. 4. Breathable Dust Cover

    Drape cheesecloth across the mouth of the vessel and tie snugly around the rim with kitchen twine — letting fermentation CO₂ vent freely while blocking fruit flies and dust.

  5. 5. Label Date & Ferment 120 Hours

    Write today's date and time on painter's tape on the vessel and ferment undisturbed at room temperature for strictly 5 full days (120 hours).

Non-Negotiable Fermentation Rule Every piece of tomato, chili, and garlic must stay 100% below the waterline across all 5 days — plastic wrap touching liquid + light top weight prevents exposed oxygen pockets where mold grows.
Phase 2

Lifting Solids & High-Speed Emulsification

  1. 6. Lift Solids Out (Leave Brine Behind)

    Once 120 hours complete, use a slotted spoon to lift all fermented solids (tomatoes, chilies, garlic) straight into a high-speed blender jar. Do NOT pour the leftover liquid brine into the blender.

  2. 7. Add Fat & Umami Binder

    Add 2 tablespoons (25 g) extra-virgin olive oil and 1 tablespoon tomato paste directly onto the drained fermented produce.

  3. 8. High-Speed Emulsify

    Blend on high speed until completely smooth, glossy, and tightly emulsified.

  4. 9. Final Salt Check

    Taste the finished sauce. Depending on how much brine the tomatoes absorbed over 5 days, add a tiny pinch of salt only if needed.

Chef's High-Heat Note To unlock complex lacto-fermented umami while keeping its bright fermented acidity intact, bake this sauce briefly at blistering pizza-oven heat — pair it directly with our Neapolitan Pizza.
