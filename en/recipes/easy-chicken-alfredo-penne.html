Easy Chicken Alfredo Penne

Easy Chicken Alfredo Penne — straight from Tasty. When comfort cravings hit, steaming bowls of creamy pasta reign supreme: tender herb-seared chicken cubes and al dente penne coated in a velvety, budget-friendly milk-and-Parmesan sauce built right in the same skillet — rated 97% would make again.

Ingredients

Serves 6. Metric equivalents: 1.5 lbs ≈ 680 g, 16 oz = 454 g.

🍗 Chicken & Pasta
Unsalted butter2 tbsp
Boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cubed1.5 lbs (~680 g)
Dried oregano½ tsp
Dried basil½ tsp
Kosher Salt½ tsp
Freshly ground black pepper½ tsp
Penne pasta, cooked & drained16 oz (454 g)
Fresh parsley, chopped¼ cup
Shredded Parmesan cheese (for finishing)¼ cup
🥛 Milk-Roux Alfredo Sauce
Unsalted butter2 tbsp
Garlic, minced4 cloves
All-purpose flour3 tbsp
Milk2 cups
Kosher Salt½ tsp
Freshly ground black pepper½ tsp
Dried oregano½ tsp
Dried basil½ tsp
Shredded Parmesan cheese½ cup

Estimated nutrition per serving (source): 396 kcal, 15 g fat, 31 g carbs, 1 g fiber, 4 g sugar, 32 g protein.

Key structural difference vs. our Fettuccine Alfredo page: this weeknight sauce thickens with a butter-flour roux + milk rather than heavy whipping cream, and both chicken and sauce cook sequentially in one skillet — fast, high-protein, and pantry-friendly.

Phase 1

Searing the Herb-Seasoned Chicken

  1. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat, add the cubed chicken breasts, and season with salt, pepper, dried oregano, and dried basil. Sauté for 8 to 10 minutes until golden and cooked through, then transfer chicken out to a plate.

Phase 2

Building the Roux Sauce in the Same Pan

  1. Return the same skillet to medium heat, melt 2 tablespoons butter, and stir in the minced garlic just until fragrant and beginning to soften.

  2. Stir in the flour half at a time until absorbed into a smooth paste, then pour in the milk gradually in stages, whisking constantly between additions until smooth and thickened.

  3. Season with salt, black pepper, oregano, and basil, then stir in ½ cup shredded Parmesan just until melted and silky.

Lump-Free Roux Rule Adding flour in two halves and streaming milk in gradually while whisking between splashes prevents lumps completely; Parmesan melts in right at the end.
Phase 3

Tossing & Serving Warm

  1. Pour the hot sauce over the cooked penne in a large serving bowl, add the seared chicken, and toss well to coat. Scatter in the fresh parsley and remaining ¼ cup Parmesan and toss once more.

  2. Serve immediately while piping hot. Mangia!

Finish

Tasty Customization Tips, Alfredo Comparison & Source

  1. Source Highlights (Tasty Tips)

    • Dial flour down slightly for a looser, silkier sauce coat 🥛🍝
    • Add extra shredded Parmesan for an ultra-cheesy pull 🧀😍
    • Bump up the minced garlic for bolder punch 🧄💥
    • Fold in steamed broccoli florets or baby spinach for color & greens 🌱🥦
    • Double the sauce batch if your household loves extra-saucy pasta! 🍜✨

  2. How Does This Differ from Momsdish's Fettuccine Alfredo Here?

    Both carry the Alfredo name via two distinct culinary mechanics: Momsdish's version emulsifies heavy whipping cream + butter + Parmesan directly without starch, whereas Tasty's penne builds a classic butter-flour béchamel/mornay base with milk + Parmesan — ideal any night you don't keep heavy cream in the fridge.
