Easy Chicken Alfredo Penne

Tasty's 97%-Approved Weeknight Classic — herb-seared chicken cubes and ridged penne coated in a silky flour-and-milk Parmesan Alfredo sauce with zero heavy cream required (396 kcal & 32 g protein per serving).

Servings
6 People
Total Time
33 Mins (15 Prep + 18 Cook)
Chicken Sear
8–10 Mins
Skill Level
Easy
1 · Sear Chicken 2 · One-Pan Milk Roux Sauce 3 · Toss & Serve

Ingredients

Serves 6. Source: Tasty — Easy Chicken Alfredo Penne.

Chicken & Pasta
Unsalted butter + Cubed boneless skinless chicken breast2 tbsp + 1.5 lb (680 g)
Dried oregano, dried basil, kosher salt & black pepper½ tsp each
Penne pasta, cooked & drained16 oz (454 g)
Fresh parsley + Shredded Parmesan (for finishing)¼ cup + ¼ cup
 Roux-Based Milk Alfredo Sauce
Unsalted butter + Minced garlic2 tbsp + 4 cloves
All-purpose flour + Milk3 tbsp + 2 cups
Kosher salt, black pepper, dried oregano & dried basil½ tsp each
Shredded Parmesan cheese½ cup
Step 1

Pan-Sear Herb Chicken

  1. Melt 2 tbsp butter over medium-high heat, add cubed chicken with ½ tsp each salt, pepper, oregano, and basil, cook 8–10 minutes until cooked through, and set aside.

Step 2

Whisk One-Pan Milk Alfredo

  1. In the same pan over medium heat, melt 2 tbsp butter, soften 4 minced garlic cloves, stir in 3 tbsp flour half at a time, then stream in 2 cups milk gradually while whisking constantly until smooth and thickened. Season with ½ tsp each salt, pepper, oregano, and basil, and melt in ½ cup Parmesan.

Step 3

Toss & Serve Warm

  1. Pour hot sauce over cooked penne in a large bowl, toss with chicken, finish with ¼ cup parsley and ¼ cup Parmesan, and serve warm. Source: Tasty (compare with our heavy-cream Momsdish and ultra-cheesy Belly Full versions).
