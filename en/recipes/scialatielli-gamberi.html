Scialatielli Aglio, Olio, Peperoncino e Gamberi

An elevated coastal twist on midnight aglio, olio e peperoncino: thick Amalfi fresh egg pasta tossed directly from the pot with sweet red prawns, cognac-deglazed pan juices, fresh chili, and wild fennel.

Servings
4 People
Prep Time
15 Minutes
Cook Time
15 Minutes
Skill Level
Very Easy
1 · Prawns & Pan Base 2 · Boil & Toss Scratch Scialatielli Dough Source & Pairing

Ingredients

Serves 4. Source: GialloZafferano — Le ricette di Michi.

Pasta & Prawn Condimento
Fresh egg Scialatielli pasta400 g
Fresh red prawns (Gamberi)400 g
Fresh red chili (Peperoncino)1 whole
Garlic cloves, peeled whole2 cloves
Fresh wild fennel fronds1 small bunch
Cognac1 small shot glass
Extra-virgin olive oil & sea saltTo taste (q.b.)
 Optional Scratch Scialatielli Dough
Durum wheat semolina flour400 g
Whole milk170 ml
Grated Pecorino + 1 Egg + Olive oil30 g + 1 egg + 1 tbsp
Step 1

Prawns & Cognac-Garlic Pan Base

  1. Peel and devein prawns, rinse, and pat thoroughly dry.

  2. Warm extra-virgin olive oil with 2 whole peeled garlic cloves in a wide pan for a few minutes. Add prawns; as soon as they turn pink, splash in cognac, let alcohol evaporate, stir in fresh chili and salt, and switch off heat.

Step 2

Transfer Dripping Pasta & Emulsify

  1. Boil fresh scialatielli in salted water just until al dente (a few minutes), lift straight into the pan with a pasta fork dripping with starchy water (do not colander-drain!), toss briskly over heat until glossy, fold in picked wild fennel leaves, and serve piping hot.

Scratch Pasta

Homemade Amalfi Scialatielli (Chef Cosentino 1978 Style)

  1. Fork 400 g semolina, 1 beaten egg, and 30 g Pecorino in a bowl; stream in 170 ml milk and 1 tbsp olive oil, knead smooth for 10 minutes, wrap, and rest chilled at least 30 minutes. Roll 5 mm thick, fold edges loosely toward center, slice into rustic 5 mm wide strips, dust with semolina, and boil ~5 minutes.

Pairing

Source & Wine Note

  1. Source: Le ricette di Michi (GialloZafferano). Paired classically with crisp chilled Asolo Prosecco Superiore Extra Brut DOCG.
