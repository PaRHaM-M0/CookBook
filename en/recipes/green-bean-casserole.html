Green Bean Casserole

Fresh green beans are briefly blanched, coated in a homemade mushroom-cream sauce, and baked under a crunchy topping of fried shallots and kettle-cooked potato chips.

Serves
6
Prep
About 15 minutes, plus extra time if making fried shallots from scratch
Cook
About 30 minutes
Total
About 45 minutes, excluding homemade fried shallots

Ingredients

Casserole
Vegetable oil2–3 tbsp (30–44 ml)
Mixed mushrooms, cleaned and roughly chopped½ lb (225 g)
Unsalted butter½ cup (113 g)
Medium shallots, finely chopped3
Garlic cloves, thinly sliced4
All-purpose flour2½ tbsp (35 g)
Heavy cream1 cup (240 ml)
Whole milk1 cup (240 ml)
Mushroom soup base powder, optional2½ tsp (10 g)
Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepperTo taste
Fresh green beans, trimmed and halved1 lb (450 g)
Topping
Fried shallots, homemade or store-bought1 cup (60 g)
Kettle-cooked potato chips, lightly crushed1 cup (60 g)
Homemade fried shallots
Shallots, peeled and sliced crosswise into ⅛-inch (3 mm) rounds3–5
Vegetable oil, for frying3 cups
Kosher saltTo taste
Phase 1

Fry the Shallots

  1. Prepare.

    Line a sheet pan with paper towels. Set it near the stove with a spider or slotted spoon.

  2. Heat the shallots and oil.

    Put the sliced shallots and oil in a Dutch oven or other deep, heavy pot. Set over medium-high heat. Stir frequently with long chopsticks as the oil heats.

  3. Drain.

    Once the oil is bubbling vigorously and the shallots are golden brown, transfer them immediately to the paper towels. Season lightly with salt and let cool; they will crisp as they cool. Measure out 1 cup for the topping. To save time, use 1 cup (60 g) store-bought crispy fried shallots instead.

Phase 2

Make the Mushroom Sauce

  1. Cook the mushrooms.

    Heat the vegetable oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms soften and begin to brown. Transfer to a bowl.

  2. Start the sauce.

    Add the butter to the same pan and let it melt. Whisk in the finely chopped shallots, garlic, and flour. Cook, whisking constantly, until the flour is moistened and the mixture smells fragrant.

  3. Add the dairy.

    Gradually pour in the cream and milk while whisking. Bring the sauce to a simmer and cook, whisking constantly, until it thickens.

  4. Return the mushrooms.

    Stir the mushrooms back into the sauce. Add the mushroom soup base, if using, and season with salt and pepper to taste. Remove the pan from the heat.

Phase 3

Blanch the Beans and Assemble

  1. Prepare the oven and dish.

    Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a 9 × 9-inch (23 × 23 cm) baking dish.

  2. Blanch the beans.

    Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it generously. Add the green beans and cook for 30–45 seconds, just until bright green and slightly softened. Drain thoroughly.

  3. Combine with the sauce.

    Transfer the beans to the baking dish. Pour the mushroom sauce over them and toss until evenly coated. Spread into an even layer.

  4. Add the topping.

    Mix the fried shallots with the lightly crushed potato chips. Scatter the topping evenly over the casserole.

Phase 4

Bake

  1. Bake.

    Bake for 10–15 minutes, until the topping is golden and the sauce bubbles around the edges. Watch closely; if the topping darkens too quickly, cover the dish loosely with foil.

  2. Serve.

    Let cool briefly before serving.

Substitutions and What the Video Actually Supports

  • Instead of canned cream of mushroom soup: The point of this version is to make a mushroom sauce from scratch with mushrooms, butter, flour, cream, and milk. The recipe does not give a quantity or method for replacing that sauce with canned soup, so it is not a measured one-for-one swap.
  • Mushroom soup base: This is marked optional in Joshua’s written recipe. Leave it out and season the sauce with salt and pepper to taste.
  • Fried shallots: Use store-bought crispy fried shallots to skip making them at home; use the amount listed for the topping either way.
  • Vegetable oil and duck fat: The video gives duck fat as a general example of swapping fats for frying. It is not presented there as a specific instruction for this casserole, so this version keeps vegetable oil as written.
  • Potato-chip topping: The written recipe specifies kettle-cooked potato chips with fried shallots. It does not identify another topping as a direct substitute.
