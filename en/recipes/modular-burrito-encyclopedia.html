Module 1

Tortilla Shell Engineering (Makes 6 × 30 cm / 12-Inch Burrito Tortillas)

CRITICAL DOUGH MASS CORRECTION VS. VIRAL VIDEOS Popular online burrito videos specify ~430 g total dough across 6 tortillas — yielding tiny ~70 g discs that roll paper-thin and tear open immediately under full fillings! True 30 cm (12-inch) Mission burrito tortillas require 95 to 100 g per ball (and ~110 g for hydrated sourdough); every formula below has been recalibrated upward to this exact structural standard.
Gear T1 · 12 Hours (Recalibrated Fat & Gram Mass)

Option 1: Elastic Sourdough Flour Tortillas

Ingredients: All-purpose flour: 330 g (2.75 cups) · Fine sea salt: 9 g (1.5 tsp) · Active mature bubbly sourdough starter (fed 12 hours ahead; cross-linked with Sourdough Bread): 120 g (½ cup) · Lukewarm water (38°C / 100°F): 150 ml (⅔ cup minus 1 tbsp) · Neutral vegetable oil OR melted lard / beef tallow: 65 ml / 60 g (generous ¼ cup).

Execution: Whisk flour and salt together, stir in lukewarm water, active sourdough starter, and oil/fat, then knead 5 minutes on the counter by hand until smooth, supple, and glossy. Ball up, wrap tight in plastic, and rest 1 to 2 hours at room temperature to relax gluten completely (or cold-retard up to 12 hours in the fridge, pulling out 1 hour pre-roll). Divide into 6 equal 110 g balls, roll out thin to full 30 cm (12-inch) circles, blister on a dry screaming-hot cast-iron skillet (245°C / 475°F) for 20 to 30 seconds per side until brown-speckled and billowing, and immediately stack inside a clean cotton kitchen towel to steam pliant.

Gear T2 · 45 Minutes (Softest Crack-Proof Folding Tortilla)

Option 2: Authentic Sonoran Hot-Water Flour Tortillas

Ingredients: All-purpose flour: 360 g (3 cups) · Kosher salt: 8 g (1.5 tsp) · Baking powder: 3 g (¾ tsp) · Softened pork lard, beef tallow, softened butter, OR liquid oil: 70 g (5 tbsp) · Near-boiling hot water (80–85°C / 175–185°F): 195 ml (¾ cup + 1 tbsp).

Execution: Whisk flour, salt, and baking powder together, rub fat/oil thoroughly into flour with fingertips until coarse damp crumbs form, then dump in all 80–85°C hot water at once — stirring first with a fork until safe to touch before kneading 4 minutes by hand until silky smooth. Wrap tightly in plastic for a full 30-minute rest, divide into 6 equal ~105 g balls, roll thin to 30 cm discs, griddle dry over hot iron 20 seconds per side, and wrap immediately inside a cotton towel.

Gear T3 · 60 Seconds (Golden Texas Shortcut)

Option 3: H-E-B Mi Tienda Raw Ready-to-Cook Flour Tortillas

Walk straight to the refrigerated deli case at H-E-B and grab raw pressed H-E-B Mi Tienda Ready-to-Cook Flour Tortillas (large Burrito / Fajita Grande size) — or fresh warm H-E-B Bakery Butter Flour Tortillas. Blister raw Mi Tienda discs directly over hot dry cast iron 30 seconds per side until puffed with golden leopard spots — virtually indistinguishable blindfolded from handmade!

Module 2

Five Distinct Protein Moods (Pick Tonight's Character)

Mood 1: Dual-Texture Crispy-Creamy Pork Carnitas

Flavor Profile & Matched Accompaniment Stack Vibe: Rich, gelatin-silky inside with torched cinnamon-orange aromatics and shattering crisp broiled edges.
Ideal Stack: Garlic-Chip Preserved-Lemon Rice + Modelo Negra Borracho Beans + Black Garlic Salsa Negra + Preserved-Lemon Pico de Gallo + Flaky-Salted Avocado Slices + Oaxaca or Monterey Jack.
Gear T1 · 16 Hours Sous-Vide (Zero Bitter White Pith)

85°C Sous-Vide Confit-Crisp Carnitas

Ingredients: 1.8 kg (4 lbs) boneless pork butt / shoulder cubed into thick 3 cm chunks · 18 g (1 tbsp) kosher salt · 1 whole cinnamon stick · 1 bay leaf · 10 peeled garlic cloves · 1 blood or Cara Cara orange (strictly 4 peeled orange zest strips with zero white pith + juice of ½ orange) · 2 tbsp fresh lime juice (never drop squeezed lime rinds inside sous-vide bags where white pith cooks bitter over 16 hours!).

Execution: Char the whole cinnamon stick briefly over an open burner flame or kitchen torch until fragrant smoke rises. Vacuum-seal salted pork chunks alongside charred cinnamon, bay leaf, garlic, orange peel strips, and citrus juices, cooking in an 85°C (185°F) water bath for 16 hours. Coarsely shred tender pork onto a rimmed sheet pan, spoon 4 tbsp strained gelatinous bag jus over top, and roast at 200°C (400°F) for 8 to 10 minutes finishing with 2 minutes directly under the broiler so ragged tips crisp while cores stay molten.

Gear T2 · 2 Hours 15 Minutes (Classic Michoacán Oven Braise)

Tight-Packed Low-Oil Oven Carnitas

Pack the exact same seasoned 4 cm pork cubes and aromatics tightly with zero air gaps into a compact Dutch oven or deep baking pan alongside 60 ml (¼ cup) vegetable oil and juice of 1 whole orange. Seal drum-tight with heavy foil and braise at 140°C (275°F) for 2 hours; reduce strained cooking liquid down to a glossy syrup in a skillet, toss with shredded pork, and broil 5 minutes until crisp-edged.

Gear T3 · 10 Minutes

Express H-E-B Mi Tienda Shortcut

Spread 1 pack of H-E-B Mi Tienda Seasoned Pork Carnitas across a sheet pan, drizzle with 2 tbsp fresh orange juice and 1 tbsp oil, and broil 8 minutes until edges caramelize crisp.

Mood 2: Char-Seared Medium-Rare Carne Asada Steak

Flavor Profile & Matched Accompaniment Stack Vibe: Smoky charred crust, rosy medium-rare steak juice, bright citrus, dried chilies, and deep hidden umami.
Ideal Stack: Buttered Cilantro Dual-Citrus Rice + Silky Butter-Mounted Black Beans + Cool Avocado-Lime Crema + Black Garlic Salsa Negra + Pico de Gallo + Aged Manchego & Cotija (California Upgrade: tuck a handful of hot crispy French fries right down the middle!).
Gear T1 (6–12h Marinade) & Gear T2 (1h Marinade) + 15 Mins Sear

Food-Lab Carne Asada with Reserved Clean Finishing Sauce (Kenji López-Alt Method)

Beef: 1.1 kg (2.5 lbs) Outside Skirt Steak (#1 choice) or Flank Steak. Blended Marinade Emulsion: 3 seeded dried Ancho chilies + 3 seeded dried Guajillo chilies · 2 canned Chipotle chilies in Adobo · 180 ml (¾ cup) fresh orange juice · 30 ml (2 tbsp) fresh lime juice · 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce · 20 ml (1.5 tbsp) fish sauce (e.g., Red Boat — invisible glutamates supercharging beefiness!) · 25 g (2 tbsp) dark brown sugar · 30 ml olive/canola oil · 6 garlic cloves · 1 tbsp toasted cumin seeds + 1 tsp toasted coriander seeds · ½ bunch fresh cilantro stems & leaves.

Execution: Toast dried chilies 20 seconds in microwave or 60 seconds in dry skillet until pliable, then blend smooth with all marinade ingredients. BEFORE touching raw beef, pour off and reserve 60 ml (¼ cup) clean marinade aside in a small bowl! Coat steak in remaining marinade for 1 hour (Gear T2) or 6 to 12 hours refrigerated (Gear T1). Wipe excess wet marinade off with paper towels, season with salt, and sear hard over blazing lump charcoal or 260°C (500°F) cast iron for strictly 3 to 4 minutes per side until charred mahogany outside and 54°C (130°F Medium-Rare) inside (Serious Eats Food Lab). Rest 5 minutes, chop fine strictly against the grain, and toss immediately in that reserved 60 ml fresh clean sauce!

Gear T3 · 12 Minutes

Express H-E-B Prime 1 Butcher Counter Shortcut

Sear 900 g butcher-counter H-E-B Prime 1 Marinated Beef Fajita Skirt Steak over screaming-hot cast iron 3.5 minutes per side, rest 5 minutes, chop fine across the grain, and finish on the board with juice of ½ lime and chopped cilantro.

Mood 3: Gelatin-Rich Slow-Braised Roast Beef / Barbacoa

Flavor Profile & Matched Accompaniment Stack Vibe: Deep, warming clove-chili aromatics, spoon-tender beef threads swimming in rich collagen beef jus.
Ideal Stack: Garlic-Chip Rice (drinks up rich jus!) + Buttered Black Beans + 15-Minute Quick-Pickled Red Onions + Avocado Crema + Stretchy Oaxaca / Monterey Jack + Quesaburrito Crispy Exterior Cheese Crust.
Gear T1 · 4-Hour 150°C Oven Braise

Food-Lab Barbacoa via Separately Seared Oxtails + Unseared Whole Chuck

Ingredients: 1.4 kg (3 lbs) whole beef chuck eye roast (left whole AND completely unseared!) · 450 g (1 lb) bone-in beef oxtails · 2 dried Ancho + 2 Guajillo chilies steeped soft in 500 ml boiling Homemade Chicken Broth / beef stock · 4 Chipotle chilies in Adobo + 2 tbsp adobo sauce · 1 sliced onion · 6 garlic cloves · 2 tsp cumin · ½ tsp ground cloves (the secret authentic soul of Mexican barbacoa!) · 2 tsp Mexican oregano · 60 ml (¼ cup) apple cider vinegar · 10 ml fish sauce or Worcestershire · 2 bay leaves.

Execution: Brown salted bone-in oxtails aggressively for 8 minutes in hot cast iron and set aside — do NOT brown the chuck roast itself so its exterior muscle fibers never toughen dry! (Serious Eats Barbacoa). Soften onion & garlic 10 minutes, bloom spices 30 seconds, deglaze with cider vinegar & chipotles, blend smooth with steeped chilies & stock, then return puree to Dutch oven with raw whole chuck, browned oxtails, and bay leaves. Braise lid-ajar 4 hours at 150°C (300°F); shred meat, fold with half the reduced gravy, and skim crimson top fat to crisp your tortillas Quesaburrito-style!

Gear T2 · 55 Minutes Pressure Braise

Instant Pot Electric Pressure Edition

Blend the identical chile-clove braising sauce, cut chuck into 6 cm chunks, cook 50 minutes on High Pressure + 15 minutes Natural Release, shred beef, and simmer sauce 10 minutes on Sauté until syrupy before folding together.

Gear T3 · 10 Minutes

Express Deli Roast Beef / Texas Brisket Glaze

Pick up 680 g slightly thick-sliced Boar's Head London Broil Roast Beef from the deli counter OR H-E-B True Texas BBQ Chopped/Sliced Brisket. Reduce 120 ml beef bone broth + 1 tbsp chipotle adobo sauce + ½ tsp cumin + tiny pinch ground cloves + 1 tsp cider vinegar for 3 minutes into a glossy glaze, kill heat, and fold sliced beef through for 30 seconds!

Mood 4: Smoky Umami Dried-Mushroom Adobo Chicken Thighs

Flavor Profile & Matched Accompaniment Stack Vibe: Charred smoky chipotle heat, juicy dark meat, and deep natural mushroom glutamates without red-meat heaviness.
Ideal Stack: Buttered Cilantro Dual-Citrus Rice + Silky Black Beans + Fresh Avocado Slices with Flaky Sea Salt + Roasted Tomatillo Salsa Verde + Red Hot Sauce + Manchego & Cotija.
Gear T1 (24h Cold Marinade) & Gear T2 (30m Counter Marinade)

Boneless Thighs in Rehydrated Shiitake-Chipotle Emulsion

Ingredients: 900 g (2 lbs) boneless, skinless chicken thighs · 2 seeded Ancho + 2 seeded Guajillo chilies · 15 g (5–6 whole caps) dried Shiitake or Oyster mushrooms · one 200 g (7 oz) can Chipotle in Adobo · 4 g coriander seeds + 4 g cumin seeds (toasted 60s) · 3 g (1 tsp) cinnamon · 12 g (2 tsp) kosher salt · juice of 1 lime · 60 ml neutral oil.

Execution: Steep dried chilies + dried mushrooms 15 minutes in 350 ml boiling water. Grind toasted seeds fine, blend steeped chilies + mushrooms + half the steeping liquor (175 ml) + chipotles + cinnamon + salt + lime smooth, streaming 60 ml oil in until glossy. Marinate thighs 24 hours cold (T1) or 30 minutes ambient (T2), char 4 minutes per side on screaming cast iron, then finish 5–7 minutes in a 200°C (400°F) oven strictly to 77–80°C (170–175°F) internal (collagen in chicken thighs melts silky right at 175°F — far juicier than 165°F!). Rest 5 minutes and cube.

Gear T3 · 8 Minutes

Express H-E-B Rotisserie Flash

Pull dark and white meat off a warm H-E-B Flaming Bird / Savory Rotisserie Chicken and sizzle 3 minutes in a hot skillet with 2 tbsp chipotle adobo sauce, ½ tsp cumin, splash of broth, and juice of ½ fresh lime until smoky-caramelized.

Mood 5: Crispy Baja Gulf Shrimp OR Steakhouse King Oyster Mushrooms

Flavor Profile & Matched Accompaniment Stack (10–15 Mins Across All 3 Gears!) Vibe: Crisp, vibrant, coastal-fresh lightness whenever skipping heavy meat.
Ideal Stack: Dual-Citrus Cilantro Rice + Black Beans + Finely Shaved Crunchy Cabbage + Avocado-Lime Crema + Pico de Gallo + Fermented Red Hot Sauce + Monterey Jack.

  1. Option 5A: Cornstarch-Crisped Garlic-Lime Gulf Shrimp (10 Mins)

    Pat 700 g peeled large Gulf shrimp bone-dry and toss with 1 tbsp cornstarch (the secret behind snappy, never-rubbery crust!), salt, smoked paprika, and garlic powder. Sear in hot cast iron with 30 g butter, 4 grated garlic cloves, chili flakes, and lime zest for strictly 90 seconds per side, finishing off-heat with fresh lime juice.

  2. Option 5B: Steakhouse Salsa-Negra Glazed Mushrooms (15 Mins)

    Sear 450 g thick-sliced Shiitake & King Oyster mushrooms untouched on blazing cast iron until deep steakhouse brown, glaze off-heat with 2 tbsp Black Garlic Salsa Negra, and roll alongside hot crispy roasted diced potatoes!

Module 3

Starch & Legume Architecture (Rice & Beans)

  1. Rice Option A-1: Crispy Garlic-Chip & Preserved-Lemon Rice (Built for Pork & Barbacoa — 25 Mins)

    Cook 380 g (2 cups) rinsed medium-grain rice with 480 ml water in a rice cooker. Meanwhile, fry 5 thinly sliced garlic cloves slowly in 60 ml oil over medium-low heat for 3 minutes until golden-crisp chips; strain, reserving garlic oil. Fluff hot rice with 2 tbsp garlic oil, all crispy garlic chips, 30 ml fresh lime juice, zest of 1 lime, 1 tbsp finely minced preserved lemon rind, and salt.

  2. Rice Option A-2: Buttered Cilantro Dual-Citrus Rice (Built for Steak, Chicken & Shrimp — 20 Mins)

    Fold hot cooked rice with 30 g butter, fresh grated zest of 1 green lime + 1 yellow lemon, juice of 2 limes, ½ cup chopped fresh cilantro, and salt. (3-Minute Gear T3 Shortcut: microwave 2 pouches ready Jasmine/White rice and toss hot with butter, dual citrus zest, lime juice, and cilantro!)

  3. Beans Option B-1: Modelo Negra Borracho Pinto Beans (T1: 2.5h Pot / T2: 40m Pressure)

    Cook 450 g overnight-soaked pinto beans tender (or 30m High Pressure unsoaked in Instant Pot!). Crisp 225 g chopped bacon in pot; sauté 1 diced onion, 5 garlic cloves, and 2 sliced seeded/unseeded jalapeños 3 minutes in bacon fat. Stir in 4 g cumin, 8 g smoked paprika, 15 g brown sugar, and 2 dried Árbol chilies; add cooked pintos, one 400 g can crushed tomatoes, one 355 ml bottle Mexican dark lager (Modelo Negra), and 4 g fresh oregano, simmering 15 minutes until rich and glossy; finish with 15 g chopped cilantro (scoop strictly with a slotted spoon at assembly so broth never wets tortillas!).

  4. Beans Option B-2: Silky Butter-Mounted Black Beans (20 Mins) + 5-Min T3 Shortcut

    Crisp 4 chopped bacon strips and set aside; soften 1 thinly sliced onion, 4 garlic cloves, and 1 sliced jalapeño in bacon fat; add 2 rinsed cans black beans + 120 ml Chicken Broth and boil briskly until 85% evaporated. Pull off heat, swirl in 30 g cold butter until glossy black velvet, and fold crisp bacon back in. (5-Min Gear T3 Shortcut: warm 1 drained can H-E-B Charro Beans or Refried Black Beans with 15 g butter + pinch of cumin.)

Module 4

Salsa, Fermentation & Cooling Emulsion Lab

MANDATORY SIGNATURE ACROSS ALL GEARS (15 MINS — KEEPS 1 MONTH CHILLED) 3. Black Garlic Salsa Negra: Warm 240 ml (1 cup) neutral oil, 18 g seeded dried Chile de Árbol, and 28 g seeded dried Guajillo chilies in a saucepan over medium heat for 3 minutes until toasted dark and fragrant. Drop heat low, add 10 peeled fresh garlic cloves, 15 peeled black garlic cloves, 15 g light brown sugar, 30 ml white vinegar, and 4 g ground cumin, simmering gently 2 to 3 minutes to mellow raw garlic bite (Joshua Weissman Reference). Cool 10 minutes, season with salt, and blend into black silk.

  1. 1. Quick & 7-Day Preserved Meyer Lemons

    [Gear T1 — 7-Day Ferment]: Mix 216 g kosher salt + 36 g sugar; quarter 7 Meyer lemons lengthwise stopping 1 cm shy of base, pack interiors generously with salt-sugar cure, press down hard inside a sterile 1 L jar until natural lemon juice submerges fruit completely, cover with cheesecloth, and cure 7 days ambient out of sunlight (rinse pulp away before mincing softened peel only).
    [Gears T2 & T3 — 0 Minutes]: Grab a jar of ready Preserved Lemons from the Mediterranean aisle at Central Market or Phoenicia Bakery in Austin (lasts a year in the fridge!).

  2. 2. Lacto-Fermented Fresno Red Hot Sauce

    [Gear T1 — 7-Day Vacuum Ferment]: Vacuum-seal 450 g sliced red Fresno chilies with strictly 14 g kosher salt (3% weight) and ferment 7 days ambient until pillow-inflated; blend Day 8 on high with 30 g sugar, 80 ml white vinegar, and 0.5 g Xanthan Gum until velvety.
    [Gear T2 — 10-Min Simmer]: Simmer 450 g Fresnos with 80 ml vinegar, 100 ml water, 14 g salt, and 30 g sugar for 8 minutes; high-speed blend with 0.5 g xanthan gum.
    [Gear T3 — Local Austin Pride]: Grab Yellowbird Habanero or Yellowbird Serrano straight off any H-E-B shelf — born right here in Austin!

  3. 4. Fresh Acidic Crunch (10–15 Mins — Pick One or Both)

    Preserved-Lemon Pico de Gallo: 3 seeded finely diced Roma tomatoes + ½ finely diced red onion + 1 seeded minced Serrano + 2 grated garlic cloves + 2 tbsp minced preserved lemon rind + juice of 2 limes + 2 tbsp cilantro + salt.
    15-Minute Quick-Pickled Red Onions (Essential on Barbacoa & Carnitas): Submerge 1 thinly sliced red onion in hot 120 ml apple cider vinegar + 120 ml water + 1 tbsp sugar + 1 tsp salt for 15 minutes until electric pink and snappy.

  4. 5. 2-Minute Silky Avocado-Lime Crema

    Immersion-blend 1 ripe avocado + 60 g (¼ cup) Mexican Crema or sour cream + juice of 1 lime + fresh cilantro stems + salt for 30 seconds until smooth green silk.

Module 5

Blowout-Proof Folding Architecture & Cast-Iron Crispy Cheese Seal

  1. 1. Mandatory 10-Second Tortilla Steam Pliability

    Cold or lukewarm flour tortillas crack immediately under tension! Warm every 30 cm tortilla 10 full seconds on hot iron or steam right before filling until stretchy.

  2. 2. Engineered Lower-Third Layering (Leave Clean 4 cm Side Borders)

    • Layer 1 (Oil Moisture Shield): Spread 1.5 tbsp Black Garlic Salsa Negra horizontally across the lower third.
    • Layer 2 (Starch Sponge): Lay a neat strip of hot aromatic rice right atop the salsa to drink meat drips.
    • Layer 3 (Hot Savory Spine): Mound generous hot protein alongside well-drained beans.
    • Layer 4 (Interior Cheese Glue): Scatter shredded Manchego + Cotija (or Oaxaca / Monterey Jack) directly over piping-hot meat.
    • Layer 5 (Cold Bright Contrast): Drained Pico de Gallo or pickled pink onions + flaky-salted avocado slices / chilled crema stripe + dashes of red hot sauce.

  3. 3. Airtight San Francisco Mission Fold

    Fold left and right tortilla flanks inward roughly 5 cm first; lift bottom flap up and completely over the filling mound with both thumbs, sweep four fingers firmly backward pulling filling tight against your chest to purge trapped air pockets, and roll forward cleanly resting seam-side down.

  4. 4. Signature Finale — Cast-Iron Crispy Cheese-Crusted Seal

    Set your cast-iron skillet over medium heat, scatter a 20 g rectangle of shredded sharp Cheddar or Monterey Jack directly onto bare hot iron, and immediately lay your rolled burrito seam-side DOWN right onto melting cheese! Sizzle undisturbed 90 seconds until molten cheese laces into a golden-brown crispy armor welding the seam shut permanently, then flip 60 seconds on the top face.

  5. 5. Non-Negotiable 2-Minute Foil Steam Equilibrium

    Wrap hot crisped burritos tightly in aluminum foil and wait strictly 2 full minutes before slicing diagonally at 45° — peeling foil down progressively from top as you eat.

Module 6

Food Science Notes (Why Every Step Works)

  1. Why Sear Oxtails Hard While Leaving Chuck Roast 100% Raw in Barbacoa?

    Per Food Lab testing, hard-searing a whole chuck roast tightens exterior protein fibers permanently dry even after 4 hours submerged; searing collagen-rich oxtails alongside raw whole chuck injects 100% of roasted Maillard pyrazines and bone gelatin into the sauce without drying a millimeter of chuck (Serious Eats).

  2. 85°C Hot-Water Starch Chemistry in Sonoran Tortillas

    Pouring 80–85°C water onto fat-coated flour partially pre-gelatinizes wheat starch while denaturing elastic glutenin recoil — letting dough roll paper-thin without snapping back or turning chewy once cooled.

  3. Thermodynamics of the 2-Minute Foil Rest

    Trapped steam inside foil marries crisp exterior cheese armor into warm flour skin, equilibrates icy avocado against hot meat, and melts center cheese into structural mortar so nothing spills out on diagonal cut.
