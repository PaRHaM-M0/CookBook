Module 1

The Tortilla Matrix (6 True 30 cm / 12-in Burrito Wraps)

Critical Weight CalibrationViral video recipes often total ~430 g dough, tearing instantly under 600 g fillings! True 12-inch burrito tortillas demand 95–110 g per dough ball — all formulas below are calibrated accordingly.
Gear T1 · 12 Hours

Option 1: Elastic Sourdough Flour Tortillas

Mix 330 g AP flour + 9 g fine sea salt; add 150 ml lukewarm water (38°C/100°F), 120 g peak active sourdough starter (related to our Sourdough Bread), and 65 ml (60 g) neutral oil or melted lard/tallow. Knead smooth 5 mins, rest wrapped 1–2h ambient (or 12h chilled + 1h temper), divide into 6 × 110 g balls, roll 30 cm thin, blister dry at 245°C (475°F) for 20–30s/side, and steam-stack inside a clean towel.

Gear T2 · 45 Minutes (Zero Crack Elasticity)

Option 2: Sonoran Hot-Water Dough

Rub 70 g softened lard/tallow/butter/oil into 360 g AP flour + 8 g kosher salt + 3 g baking powder until sandy; pour in 195 ml near-boiling water (80–85°C / 175–185°F) all at once, fork together, knead silky 4 mins, rest wrapped 30 full minutes, divide into 6 balls, roll 30 cm thin, and blister 20s/side on hot dry cast iron.

Gear T3 · 60 Seconds (Texas Gold Shortcut)

Option 3: H-E-B Mi Tienda Ready-to-Cook Tortillas

Grab refrigerated uncooked H-E-B Mi Tienda Ready-to-Cook Flour Tortillas (Burrito/Fajita Grande) (or warm H-E-B Bakery Fresh Butter Flour Tortillas) and puff raw sheets 30s/side on hot cast iron.

Module 2

Choose Your Burrito Personality (5 Protein Moods)

Mood 1: Dual-Texture Pork Carnitas

Gear T1 · 16h Sous-Vide

Torch-Charred Cinnamon Sous-Vide Carnitas

Char 1 whole cinnamon stick over an open flame/torch until smoking; vacuum-seal with 1.8 kg (4 lbs) 3 cm cubed pork butt, 18 g kosher salt, 1 bay leaf, 10 garlic cloves, 4 pith-free blood/Cara Cara orange peel strips + juice of ½ orange, and 2 tbsp fresh lime juice (no bitter white pith!). Cook 16h at 85°C (185°F), shred coarsely, toss with 4 tbsp strained bag gelatin jus, and crisp 8–10 mins at 200°C + 2m broil.

Gear T2 · 2h 15m Traditional Michoacán Braise

Low-Oil Oven Confit Carnitas

Pack tight in a small pan with 60 ml oil + juice of 1 orange under tight foil for 2h at 140°C (275°F); reduce pan liquid syrupy, toss with shredded pork, and broil 5 mins crisp.

Gear T3 · 10 Minutes

H-E-B Mi Tienda Express

Spread H-E-B Mi Tienda Seasoned Pork Carnitas with 2 tbsp fresh orange juice + 1 tbsp oil and broil 8 mins until crispy-edged.

Mood 2: Smoky Medium-Rare Carne Asada Steak

Gear T1 (6–12h) & Gear T2 (1h) + 15m Sear

Kenji's Reserved-Sauce Carne Asada

Toast & blend 3 seeded Anchos + 3 seeded Guajillos + 2 Chipotles in Adobo + 180 ml orange juice + 30 ml lime juice + 30 ml soy sauce + 20 ml Red Boat fish sauce + 25 g dark brown sugar + 30 ml oil + 6 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp toasted cumin + 1 tsp toasted coriander + ½ bunch cilantro. Reserve 60 ml clean sauce BEFORE touching raw beef! Marinate 1.1 kg Outside Skirt Steak 1h (T2) or 6–12h (T1), wipe dry, salt, char at 260°C (500°F) 3–4m/side to 54°C (130°F) (Serious Eats), rest 5m, dice across grain, and toss with the 60 ml fresh reserved sauce!

Gear T3 · 12 Minutes

H-E-B Prime 1 Skirt Express

Sear 900 g H-E-B Prime 1 Marinated Beef Fajita Skirt Steak 3.5m/side on blazing cast iron, rest 5m, chop, and hit with fresh lime + cilantro.

Mood 3: Gelatin-Rich Slow-Braised Roast Beef / Barbacoa

Gear T1 · 4 Hours Oven Braise

Browned Oxtail + Unseared Whole Chuck Barbacoa

Hard-brown 450 g bone-in oxtails 8 mins while keeping 1.4 kg whole Chuck Eye Roast completely UNSEARED so outer fibers never dry out (Serious Eats)! Sauté onion + 6 garlic cloves, bloom 2 tsp cumin + ½ tsp ground cloves + 2 tsp Mexican oregano, blend smooth with 2 Anchos + 2 Guajillos steeped in 500 ml hot stock + 4 Chipotles + 60 ml apple cider vinegar + 10 ml fish/Worcestershire sauce, braise lid-cracked 4h at 150°C (300°F), shred in gelatin jus, and save red surface fat for toasting tortillas!

Gear T2 · 55m Instant Pot

High-Pressure Barbacoa

Cut chuck into 6 cm chunks, cook in sauce 50m High Pressure + 15m Natural Release, shred, and reduce jus 10m on Sauté.

Gear T3 · 10 Minutes

Deli Roast Beef / Texas Brisket Glaze

Reduce 120 ml beef bone broth + 1 tbsp Adobo sauce + ½ tsp cumin + pinch cloves + 1 tsp cider vinegar for 3 mins, kill heat, and swirl 680 g Boar's Head London Broil or H-E-B True Texas BBQ Brisket in warm glaze 30s.

Mood 4: Smoky Umami Dried-Mushroom Adobo Chicken

Gear T1 (24h) & Gear T2 (30m)

175°F Charred Chicken Thighs

Steep 2 Anchos + 2 Guajillos + 15 g dried oyster/shiitake mushrooms in 350 ml boiling water 15m; blend with 175 ml soaking liquid, 200 g can Chipotle in Adobo, toasted coriander (4 g) & cumin (4 g), cinnamon (3 g), kosher salt (12 g), juice of 1 lime, and 60 ml oil. Marinate 900 g boneless thighs, char 4m/side on cast iron, and finish 5–7m at 200°C to 77–80°C (170–175°F) for silky collagen breakdown.

Gear T3 · 8 Minutes

H-E-B Rotisserie Flash

Sauté shredded H-E-B Flaming Bird / Savory Rotisserie Chicken 3 mins with 2 tbsp Chipotle Adobo, ½ tsp cumin, splash broth, and juice of ½ lime.

Mood 5: Crispy Garlic-Chile Gulf Shrimp OR Steakhouse Mushrooms

  1. Seafood (10m): Toss 700 g dry Large Gulf Shrimp with 1 tbsp cornstarch, salt, smoked paprika & garlic powder; sear 90s/side in 30 g butter + 4 grated garlic cloves + chili flakes + lime zest, finish with lime juice.

  2. Steakhouse Veg (15m): Hard-sear 450 g thick Shiitake & King Oyster slabs undisturbed until deep brown, glaze with 2 tbsp Black Garlic Salsa Negra, and pair with crispy roasted cubed potatoes!

Module 3

Rice & Beans Architecture

  1. Rice Option 1 — Garlic-Chip & Preserved Lemon (25m): Cook 380 g medium-grain rice in 480 ml water; crisp 5 sliced garlic cloves in 60 ml oil 3m, fold 2 tbsp garlic oil + all garlic chips + 30 ml lime juice + zest of 1 lime + 1 tbsp minced preserved lemon peel into hot rice.

  2. Rice Option 2 — Buttered Cilantro Dual-Citrus (20m / T3 3m): Fold 30 g butter, zest of 1 lime + 1 yellow lemon, juice of 2 limes, ½ cup cilantro & salt into hot rice (T3: microwave Jasmine rice pouches!).

  3. Beans Option 1 — Modelo Negra Borracho Beans (T1 2.5h / T2 40m Pressure): 450 g pintos simmered with 225 g crisped bacon, onion, 5 garlic cloves, 2 sliced jalapeños, cumin, smoked paprika, brown sugar, 2 Árbol chiles, 400 g crushed tomatoes, 1 bottle (355 ml) Modelo Negra, fresh oregano & cilantro (drain well with slotted spoon!).

  4. Beans Option 2 — Silky Butter-Mounted Black Beans (20m / T3 5m): Simmer 2 drained cans black beans + 120 ml chicken broth over bacon-fat aromatics until 85% reduced, swirl in 30 g cold butter off heat (T3: warm H-E-B Charro / Refried Black Beans with 15 g butter & cumin).

Module 4

Salsas, Ferments & Coolers

Mandatory in ALL Gears — 15-Minute Black Garlic Salsa NegraToast 18 g seeded Árbol + 28 g seeded Guajillo in 240 ml neutral oil 3 mins over medium heat; drop low, add 10 raw garlic cloves, 15 black garlic cloves, 15 g light brown sugar, 30 ml white vinegar & 4 g cumin, simmer gently 2–3 mins (Joshua Weissman Ref), cool 10 mins, and blend silky smooth with salt (keeps 1 month chilled!).

  1. Preserved Meyer Lemons: T1 7-day jar cure (7 quarter-slit Meyer lemons packed with 216 g kosher salt + 36 g sugar); T2/T3 buy ready jar at Central Market / Phoenicia Bakery.

  2. Red Hot Sauce: T1 7-day vacuum lacto-ferment (450 g Fresno chiles + 14 g [3%] kosher salt, blended Day 8 with 30 g sugar + 80 ml white vinegar + 0.5 g Xanthan Gum); T2 8-min simmered Fresno blend; T3 grab local Austin Yellowbird Habanero / Serrano!

  3. Fresh Acid & 2-Min Crema: Preserved-Lemon Pico de Gallo (seeded Roma, red onion, serrano, garlic, preserved peel, lime, cilantro) + 15-Min Pickled Red Onions + Silky Avocado-Lime Crema (1 ripe avocado + 60 g Mexican crema/sour cream + juice of 1 lime + cilantro blended 30s).

Module 5

Tear-Proof Mission Fold & Crispy Cheese-Crusted Seal

  1. 10-Second Steam Pliability: Always heat tortilla 10s first so gluten stretches zero-tear. Layer across the lower third (4 cm border clean): 1.5 tbsp Salsa Negra → hot Rice → hot Meat & drained Beans → shredded Cheese → cold Pico/Pickled Onion + Crema/Avocado + Hot Sauce.

  2. Mission Fold + Cast-Iron Cheese Seal: Fold left & right sides 5 cm inward, roll bottom flap tightly up and backward squeezing out all air, scatter a bar of 20 g shredded Cheddar / Monterey Jack directly on medium cast iron, press burrito seam-side DOWN straight onto the melting cheese for 90 seconds until golden lacy armor welds the seam shut, crisp top 60s, wrap tight in foil for 2 full minutes, and slice 45° diagonal!

Module 6

Culinary Science

  1. Browning oxtails separately prevents dry outer chuck fibers; 85°C hot water partially gelatinizes starch and relaxes glutenin for zero snap-back; and the 2-minute foil steam welds cheese, rice, and tortilla into a unified cross-section.
