Pantry Guide

Essential Chiles: Heat, Flavor & Purpose

Chile is the Spanish/Mexican ingredient name; chili is the common English spelling and also the name of the stew. Heat is only one variable: dried chiles are chosen for fruit, smoke, color, tannin, and depth. Scoville ranges vary by crop and producer.

The three-chile decisionAncho builds dark sweetness; Guajillo supplies bright red fruit and body; Chile de Árbol adds direct heat. Blending a mild flavor chile with a hotter one gives better control than using heat alone. See the Mexican chile reference.
Sauce 1 of 10

McDonald’s Sweet and Sour Sauce

⏱️ Prep 5 minutes · Cook 5 minutes · Serves 12

  1. Build the Fruit-and-Vinegar Base

    Add the apricot jam, peach jam, brown sugar, lemon juice, sherry vinegar, shirodashi, yellow mustard, and salt to taste to a small saucepan. Place over medium heat and bring to a simmer, stirring often.

  2. Whisk and Add the Slurry

    Meanwhile, whisk together the cornstarch and water in a small bowl until completely smooth. Once the sauce reaches a simmer, whisk in the cornstarch slurry and cook, whisking constantly, until thickened and glossy, about 20–30 seconds.

  3. Cool Completely

    Remove from the heat and let cool completely before serving. The sauce will continue to thicken as it cools.

Popeyes Blackened Ranch Sauce

⏱️ Prep 10 minutes · Cook 5 minutes · Serves 30

  1. Whisk Until Smooth

    In a large mixing bowl, combine the mayonnaise, crème fraîche, Dijon, buttermilk, chives, parsley, dill, paprika, onion powder, cayenne, celery powder, grated garlic, salt and pepper to taste, and MSG. Whisk until thoroughly combined and smooth.

  2. Rest the Ranch

    Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to allow the herbs and spices to bloom. For the best flavor, refrigerate overnight.

Wendy’s Honey BBQ Sauce

⏱️ Prep 5 minutes · Cook 10 minutes · Serves 16

  1. Combine the Sauce

    Combine the ketchup, honey, molasses, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, black pepper, vinegar, Worcestershire, Dijon, and liquid smoke in a small saucepan.

  2. Thin, Boil and Simmer

    Add a small splash of water to thin to your desired consistency. Bring to a gentle boil over medium heat, then reduce to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, for 3–4 minutes. Season to taste with salt.

  3. Cool and Rest

    Let cool completely before serving. For the best flavor, cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.

Sauce 5 of 10

Whataburger Spicy Ketchup

⏱️ Prep 10 minutes · Cook 10 minutes · Serves 16

  1. Char, Steam and Peel the Jalapeños

    Char the jalapeños on all sides over an open flame or under a broiler, then transfer to a bowl and cover with plastic wrap. Let steam until softened, about 5 minutes. Wipe away the charred skin with a damp paper towel. Remove and discard the stems, then transfer the jalapeños to the base of a blender.

  2. Load the Blender

    Add the ketchup, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, honey, and salt to taste.

  3. Blend Completely Smooth

    Blend on high speed until completely smooth, scraping down the sides of the blender as needed. Taste and adjust the seasoning with additional salt, vinegar, or cayenne as desired.

  4. Rest for Best Flavor

    For the best flavor, cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.

Individual source: Whataburger Spicy Ketchup

Sauce 6 of 10

Easy Mayonnaise

⏱️ Prep 5 minutes · Cook 1 minute · Serves 16

  1. Layer the Ingredients

    Add the egg yolks, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, water, and salt to a tall, narrow quart container. Pour the neutral oil over top and allow it to settle for a few seconds.

  2. Emulsify From the Bottom

    Place an immersion blender at the very bottom of the container. Blend without moving the blender until the mixture at the bottom becomes thick, creamy, and emulsified, about 10–15 seconds.

  3. Lift and Finish

    With the blender still running, slowly lift it toward the surface to incorporate the remaining oil. Continue blending until completely smooth and emulsified. Season to taste with additional salt, if needed.

Individual source: Easy Mayonnaise

Sauce 7 of 10

Taco Bell Fire Sauce

⏱️ Prep 10 minutes · Cook 10 minutes · Serves 50

  1. Rehydrate the Chiles

    Add the ancho and guajillo chiles to a small mixing bowl, then pour over the boiling water. Let sit until rehydrated and softened, about 10 minutes. The chiles should tear easily by hand. Do not discard the soaking liquid.

  2. Blend With the Reserved Liquid

    Transfer the softened chiles to the base of a blender along with the onion powder, cayenne, paprika, cumin, garlic, vinegar, salt to taste, and tomato paste. Blend on medium speed, gradually increasing to high, until as smooth as possible. With the blender running, gradually stream in the reserved chile soaking liquid until completely incorporated and smooth. Season again to taste with salt.

  3. Strain Optionally and Cool

    Optionally, for a smoother, more Taco Bell-like consistency, pass the sauce through a fine-mesh strainer. Let cool completely before refrigerating.

Individual source: Taco Bell Fire Sauce

Sauce 9 of 10

Hot Honey — Chile de Árbol

🍯 Prep 5 minutes · Cook 5 minutes · 140–150°F infusion · 15-minute rest
What is Hot Honey?Honey infused with chile: sweet first, then a clean rise of heat. It is a finishing condiment for pizza, fried chicken, biscuits, roasted vegetables, grilled meat and steak; it can also glaze wings, ribs or ham, sharpen BBQ sauce, or replace simple syrup in selected cocktails.

  1. Prep the Chiles

    Wash the 12–16 dried chiles de árbol under cold water and pat dry. Remove the stems and chop into small rings. Keep the seeds for more heat or discard them for less. Wear gloves and keep your hands away from your eyes.

  2. Warm the Honey

    Add the chiles and 1 cup honey to a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Aim for 140–150°F (60–66°C). Once the first bubbles form at the edges, hold it there for 1–2 minutes. Do not walk away.

  3. Add Vinegar and Rest

    Pull the pan off the heat. Stir in 2 tsp apple cider vinegar. Let it sit for at least 15 minutes.

  4. Transfer and Store

    Pour into a glass jar. Strain out the chiles for consistent heat, or leave them in and the honey will get spicier over time. Seal once fully cooled and store at room temperature.

Source-Tested Chile Variations
  • Guajillo: 4–6 chiles per cup; remove stems and seeds and tear into pieces. Mild, smoky, deep red.
  • Chipotle Morita: 4–6 chiles per cup. Smoky and spicy; especially suited to BBQ glazes.
  • Chiltepin: 1–2 tsp whole per cup. Very hot; use sparingly.

Recipe source: Chiles and Smoke — Hot Honey with Dried Chiles · Infusion-safety reference: Oregon State University Extension — Infusing Honey Safely

Sauce 10 of 10

Creamy Garlic Aioli Dipping Sauce

🧄 Yield about ¾ cup · Prep 10 minutes · Chill at least 30 minutes

  1. Mellow the Raw Garlic

    Finely grate the 1 small garlic clove (about 3g) into a small bowl. Stir in the 2 tsp (10mL) fresh lemon juice and leave for 5 minutes.

  2. Whisk the Sauce Smooth

    Add the ½ cup (115g) full-fat mayonnaise, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp (5mL) mild olive oil, ⅛ tsp fine salt, and a small pinch of white pepper. Whisk until completely smooth.

  3. Adjust the Consistency

    For a thicker dipping sauce, leave it as is. For a softer, more drizzle-able sauce, whisk in cold water ½ teaspoon at a time, using 1–2 tsp as needed.

  4. Chill, Taste and Adjust

    Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. Taste before serving and adjust the salt or lemon.

Garlic & Aioli Notes
  • Start with one small clove; raw garlic gets stronger as the sauce rests. Add more only after chilling and tasting.
  • Use freshly grated garlic rather than jarred minced garlic for a cleaner flavor.
  • Refrigerate in a covered container and use within 3 days.
  • This is a mayonnaise-based aioli-style sauce, not a traditional oil-and-garlic-only aioli. The Spruce Eats' linked recipe is a from-scratch garlic mayonnaise; its slow egg-and-oil emulsion is a different, more traditional approach.
Food Safety

Longer Storage Without Guesswork

Core Temperature RulesUse clean, dry, airtight containers; label and date every batch. Refrigerate perishable sauces within 2 hours and keep the refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below; keep the freezer at 0°F (-18°C) or below. The limits below are deliberately conservative home-kitchen guidance, not claims that these untested formulas are shelf-stable.

Honey safety: Never feed honey or honey-infused products to an infant under 1 year old.

Do You Need Chemical Preservatives?No—not for these home batches. Do not casually add sodium benzoate or potassium sorbate. Their effectiveness depends on validated concentration, equilibrium pH, water activity and processing; they do not turn an untested recipe into a safe shelf-stable product. Likewise, a pH reading below 4.6 is a critical commercial boundary, but by itself is not permission to bottle an untested sauce at room temperature. Use only research-tested canning formulas; otherwise refrigerate or freeze.
