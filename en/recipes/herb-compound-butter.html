Herb Compound Butter

A solid, sliceable pan sauce stored right in your fridge: unsalted butter mashed with shallots or garlic, fresh herbs, and bright lemon that melts instantaneously over hot steaks, fish, pasta, or roasted vegetables.

Yield
8 Servings
Butter Base
½ Cup · 1 Stick · 113 g
Chill Time
At Least 3 Hours
Skill Level
Easy
1 · Preparation 2 · 4 Classic Uses

Ingredients

US Stick Conversion: 1 stick = ½ cup = 4 oz = 8 tbsp = 113 g.

Herb Compound Butter
Unsalted butter, softened½ cup (1 stick / 113 g)
Minced shallots OR Microplane-grated garlic clove1 tbsp shallot or 1 clove
Fresh thyme or rosemary, finely chopped1 tbsp
Fresh parsley or chives, finely minced1 tbsp
Fresh lemon juice1 tsp
Black pepper + Fine sea salt¼ tsp + ¼ tsp (more to taste)
Method

Two-Step Preparation

  1. Mash & Emulsify

    In a bowl, mash softened butter together with shallots (or garlic), chopped fresh herbs, lemon juice, black pepper, and fine sea salt until evenly combined.

  2. Roll & Chill

    Spoon onto parchment paper or plastic wrap, shape into a tight cylinder log, twist ends shut, and chill at least 3 hours until firm enough to slice clean coins.

Applications

Four Restaurant Ways to Use It

  1. Steaks, Chicken & Seafood

    Drop a cold coin straight onto seared steak, grilled chicken, fish, or shrimp off the heat for an instant glossy pan sauce.

  2. Steamed or Roasted Vegetables

    Toss with hot asparagus, corn on the cob, baked potatoes, carrots, or broccoli.

  3. Warm Bread & Pasta

    Spread on crusty toast for instant herb-garlic bread, or swirl a spoonful into freshly drained hot pasta.

  4. Aromatic Cooking Base

    Use in place of plain butter to sauté mushrooms, onions, or shrimp with built-in garlic and herbs.
