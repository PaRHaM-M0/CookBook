Porchetta-Infused Bacon

Precision equilibrium-cured pork belly encrusted heavily in fresh thyme, rosemary, garlic paste, orange zest, chili flakes, and nutmeg — smoked slowly at 250°F to 160°F internal and chilled firm for pizza cubes or thick slabs.

Meat
4.5 lbs (2 kg) Raw Pork Belly
Fridge Cure
Exact 6 to 7 Days
Smoke / Bake
250°F (120°C) · 4–5 Hours
Internal Target
Exact 160°F (71°C)
Chill Before Slicing
6 to 12 Hours
Phase 1 · 6–7 Day Cure Phase 2 · Rinse & Porchetta Crust Phase 3 · 250°F Smoke & Chill

Ingredients & Gear

Digital scale is an absolute food-safety requirement.

Pork & Precision Cure
Raw Pork Belly (skin-on ideal, skin-off great too)4.5 lbs (2 kg)
Kosher Salt45 g exact
Dark Brown Sugar30 g exact
Prague Powder #1 (Pink Curing Salt — WEIGH EXACTLY!)5 g exact
 Porchetta Herb Rub
Fresh Thyme leaves + Fresh Rosemary leaves, finely chopped¼ cup (12 g) + ¼ cup (11 g)
Garlic smashed to paste with 1 pinch salt4 cloves
Zest of 1 whole Orange1 orange
Red Pepper Flakes + Ground Nutmeg½ tsp (1 g) + ½ tsp (1 g)
Critical Safety Warning — Prague Powder #1Weigh 5 grams on an accurate digital scale — never eyeball pink curing salt, and wash hands thoroughly immediately after handling.
Phase 1

6 to 7-Day Equilibrium Cure

  1. Mix 45 g kosher salt, 30 g dark brown sugar, and 5 g Prague Powder #1 uniformly, massage thoroughly onto every surface of the 2 kg pork belly, seal air-free inside a jumbo Ziploc bag labeled clearly with today's date, and refrigerate for 6 to 7 days until firm.

Phase 2

Cold Rinse & Heavy Herb Crust

  1. Rinse belly thoroughly under cold running water to wash off surface cure and pat completely dry with paper towels.

  2. Mince 4 garlic cloves with a pinch of coarse salt into a paste, toss with chopped thyme (12 g), rosemary (11 g), orange zest, red pepper flakes (1 g), and nutmeg (1 g), and press heavily onto all sides of the belly.

Phase 3

250°F Smoke/Oven & 6–12h Firming Chill

  1. Cook in a smoker or on a wire rack in a standard oven at 250°F (120°C) for 4 to 5 hours until thickest internal point hits 160°F (71°C).

  2. Do not slice hot! Refrigerate 6 to 12 hours so fats set rock-firm for clean dicing on our Neapolitan Pizza.
