Porchetta-Infused Bacon

Scratch Porchetta-Infused Bacon — documented step-for-step from the video specification. Raw pork belly cures for strictly 6 to 7 days under precision-weighed curing salts, gets rinsed and plastered on all faces with classic aromatic Porchetta paste (fresh thyme, rosemary, garlic paste, orange zest, crushed red pepper, nutmeg), slow-smokes or roasts at 250°F strictly to 160°F (71°C) internal, and chills 6–12 hours firm before dicing.

Pork Cut
4.5 lbs (2 kg) raw pork belly
Fridge Cure
Strictly 6 to 7 days
Roast / Smoke
250°F (120°C), ~4 to 5 hours
Target Internal
Strictly 160°F (71°C)
Chill Before Slicing
At least 6 to 12 hours
Skill Level
Advanced
Phase 1: 7-Day Cure Phase 2: Rinse & Porchetta Paste Phase 3: 250°F Smoke/Roast & Chill

Ingredients & Equipment

Curing salts MUST be measured strictly by gram weight — a digital scale is non-negotiable for food safety.

Meat
Raw pork belly (skin-on preferred; skinless works equally well)4.5 lbs (2 kg)
Precision Cure Mix (By Weight)
Kosher salt45 g
Dark brown sugar30 g
Prague Powder #1 (Pink Curing Salt — 6.25% sodium nitrite)Strictly 5 g
Aromatic Porchetta Herb Crust
Fresh thyme leaves, finely chopped¼ cup (12 g)
Fresh rosemary leaves, stemmed & finely chopped¼ cup (11 g)
Garlic cloves, crushed into paste4 cloves
Coarse salt pinch (abrasive for mashing garlic)1 pinch
Fresh grated orange zestZest of 1 whole orange
Crushed red pepper flakes½ tsp (1 g)
Ground nutmeg½ tsp (1 g)
Required Equipment
Sheet pan / rimmed tray1
Small spice bowls2
Precision digital gram scale (mandatory for nitrite safety)1
Jumbo / 2-gallon heavy zip-top bag1
Sharp chef's knife & cutting board1 set
Smoker OR standard home oven with wire rackEither
Leave-in or instant-read meat probe1
CRITICAL SAFETY WARNING — Prague Powder #1 Weigh Prague Powder #1 to strictly 5.0 grams on a gram scale — NEVER estimate curing nitrites with measuring spoons, keep safely away from table salt, and wash hands thoroughly immediately after handling.
Phase 1

6-to-7-Day Equilibrium Cure

Refrigerator cure: strictly 6 to 7 days — write start date directly on bag in marker

  1. 1. Blending the Cure

    Whisk together strictly 45 g kosher salt, 30 g dark brown sugar, and 5 g Prague Powder #1 in a small bowl until completely uniform.

  2. 2. Dredging & Massaging

    Set the raw pork belly on a rimmed sheet pan and coat every single surface — top, bottom, and all four sides — pressing and massaging firmly so maximum cure adheres.

  3. 3. Bagging & Dating

    Slide the belly plus any loose cure crystals from the tray into a jumbo zip-top bag, press out excess air, seal tight, and write today's date boldly on the bag.

  4. 4. Refrigerated Cure

    Refrigerate flat for strictly 6 to 7 days; by Day 7 the belly will feel noticeably firm to the touch.

Phase 2

Rinsing Salt & Plastering Porchetta Herb Paste

  1. 5. Thorough Cold Rinse

    Unbag the cured belly at the sink, rinse thoroughly under cold running water to wash off surface cure salt, and pat bone-dry with paper towels.

  2. 6. Mashing Garlic Paste

    Smash 4 peeled garlic cloves on your board, sprinkle with a pinch of coarse salt as grit, and smear with the flat side of your chef's knife into a smooth paste.

  3. 7. Mixing the Herb Crust

    Combine the garlic paste, finely chopped thyme, chopped rosemary, fresh orange zest, crushed red pepper flakes, and ground nutmeg in a bowl, tossing to blend.

  4. 8. Coating Every Face

    Pack and press the aromatic Porchetta herb crust firmly across the top, bottom, and all four sides of the dried pork belly.

Phase 3

250°F Low Smoke/Roast & Cold Slicing Set

250°F (120°C) for ~4–5h strictly to 160°F (71°C) internal — then 6–12h chill before cutting

  1. 9. Into the Smoker or Oven

    Set the crusted belly into a smoker preheated to 250°F (120°C) — or onto a wire rack over a foil-lined sheet pan inside a standard 250°F home oven.

  2. 10. Pulling Strictly at 160°F Internal

    Roast/smoke for roughly 4 to 5 hours until a meat probe inserted into the thickest center reads strictly 160°F (71°C).

  3. 11. Mandatory Cold Firm-Up

    Pull the finished bacon and do NOT slice hot! Refrigerate at least 6 to 12 hours so rendered collagen and pork fat set firm for razor-clean lardon cubes.
