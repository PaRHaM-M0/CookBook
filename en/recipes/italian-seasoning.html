Italian Seasoning

Balanced, aromatic 5-minute pantry staple for pasta sauces, soups, chicken marinades, and roasted vegetables — zero fillers, zero hidden sodium, 4.95 stars from 219 reviews.

Yield
8 Tablespoons (½ Cup)
Prep Time
5 Minutes
Shelf Life
Up to 6 Months Peak Aroma
Skill Level
Easy
1 · Mix & Store 2 · Custom Variations

Ingredients

Yields 8 tablespoons. Source: Spend With Pennies.

Core Blend
Dried basil2 tbsp
Dried oregano2 tbsp
Dried parsley2 tbsp
Dried rosemary1 tbsp
Dried thyme leaves1 tbsp
Red pepper flakes1 tbsp
Garlic powder1 tsp
 Optional Herb Additions
Dried marjoram + Onion powder + Ground sage1 tsp + ½ tsp + ¼ tsp
Method

Measure, Whisk & Jar

  1. Whisk all dried herbs and spices evenly in a small bowl and funnel into an airtight glass spice jar. Store in a cool, dark cabinet for up to 6 months of peak aroma.

Notes

Flavor Tuning & Cross-Recipes

  1. Adjust red pepper flakes up or down to taste, or crush rosemary needles lightly in a mortar for finer dispersion. Used directly in our Belly Full Chicken Alfredo and Lemon Chicken Orzo Soup. Source: Holly Nilsson — Spend With Pennies.
