Popeyes-Style Fried Chicken Meal

With Red Beans & Rice, Mac & Cheese, and Honey-Butter Biscuits. The bean pot needs the longest, mostly hands-off simmer, so start it first. Make the biscuits while the beans cook, prepare the macaroni and cheese near serving time, and fry the chicken last. Based on Joshua Weissman's published recipe and video.

Phase 1

Red Beans & Rice

⏱️ Start first · Uncovered 3-hour simmer + 45–60 minute finish

  1. Soften the Vegetables

    Melt the bacon fat in a large stockpot over medium heat. Add the onion, bell pepper, and garlic. Season lightly with salt and black pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables begin to soften, about 5–7 minutes.

  2. Toast the Seasonings

    Stir in the Creole seasoning, smoked paprika, and cayenne. Cook for about 1 minute, stirring, until fragrant.

  3. Add the Beans and Aromatics

    Pour in the water and add the dried beans and ham hocks. Tie the thyme and sage together with kitchen twine and add them to the pot with the bay leaves.

  4. Simmer the Beans

    Bring the pot to a boil, then lower the heat to maintain a steady, gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for 3 hours, stirring occasionally. Keep the beans covered with liquid; if the level gets low before they are tender, add hot water.

  5. Finish With Sausage and Celery

    Add the celery and andouille. Continue simmering for 45–60 minutes, until the beans are tender and the broth has thickened. The beans should be soft inside but mostly hold their shape.

  6. Finish the Pot

    Remove the ham hocks and herb bundle. Discard the bay leaves and herb stems. Pull the meat from the ham-hock bones and stir it back into the beans. Taste before adding more salt—the ham, sausage, and Creole seasoning may already contribute plenty.

  7. Prepare the Rice

    Cook enough white rice to yield 4 cups. Keep it covered and warm until serving.

Phase 2

Buttermilk Biscuits & Honey Butter

⏱️ Make while the beans simmer · 450°F oven · 15–20 minute bake

  1. Prepare the Pan and Dry Ingredients

    Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Add the flour, salt, sugar, cream of tartar, and baking powder to a food processor. Pulse briefly to combine.

  2. Cut in the Cold Butter

    Add the cubed butter and pulse until the mixture contains pea-size pieces. Stop before it becomes a uniform paste; visible bits of butter help create a flaky biscuit.

  3. Make a Rough Dough

    Transfer the flour mixture to a large bowl. Pour in the cold buttermilk while stirring with a fork. Stop as soon as a shaggy dough forms. If there is a little loose flour at the bottom, press it into the dough gently; do not knead the dough smooth.

  4. Fold the Dough

    Turn it onto a lightly floured counter and gently press it together. Roll it into a rectangle about ¾ inch (18mm) thick. Fold it into thirds like a letter, roll it out again, and repeat the fold once more. Roll the dough to ¾ inch thick.

  5. Cut and Bake

    Press a biscuit cutter straight down through the dough without twisting. Place the biscuits on the lined baking sheet. Gently press the scraps together once to cut additional biscuits. Brush the tops lightly with buttermilk and bake for 15–20 minutes, until risen and golden.

  6. Make the Honey Butter

    While the biscuits bake, warm the honey in a small saucepan over medium heat until hot and steaming, but not boiling. Remove it from the heat and whisk in the cold salted butter until smooth. Brush the honey butter over the hot biscuits when they come out of the oven, then transfer them to a wire rack.

Phase 3

Mac & Cheese

⏱️ Begin about 45 minutes before serving · Hold over very low heat

  1. Cook the Pasta

    Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it generously. Add the macaroni and cook until al dente according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.

  2. Make the Roux

    In a saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter. Whisk in the flour and cook, stirring constantly, for 45–60 seconds, until the mixture is smooth and pale yellow.

  3. Make the Cheese Sauce

    Gradually whisk in the milk and cream. Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer, whisking often, until it thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon. If it becomes too thick, whisk in more milk a splash at a time.

  4. Melt the Cheese Off the Heat

    Remove the saucepan from the heat. Add the cheddar and Gouda and stir until melted and smooth. Season with salt and a pinch of onion powder.

  5. Combine and Hold Warm

    Fold in the drained macaroni until evenly coated, then stir in the finely minced garlic. Keep the pan warm over very low heat, stirring occasionally. If the sauce tightens while waiting, loosen it with a little warm milk.

Phase 4

Fried Chicken

⏱️ Bread about 30 minutes before serving · Fry last · 350°F → 325°F

  1. Set Up the Frying Area

    Place a wire rack over a rimmed baking sheet near the stove. Use a heavy pot with ample headroom; hot oil must not come close to the rim. Heat the oil to 350°F (175°C) and clip the frying thermometer securely to the pot.

  2. Mix the Buttermilk Dip

    In a large bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, pickle juice, salt, MSG, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, Creole seasoning, and hot sauce. Add the chicken and turn the pieces to coat. Keep the bowl refrigerated if the chicken will wait before breading.

  3. Mix the Seasoned Flour

    In a separate wide bowl, whisk together the flour, MSG, garlic powder, onion powder, white pepper, Creole seasoning, and salt. Set it beside the buttermilk bowl, with a clean tray or rack ready for the breaded chicken.

  4. Bread the Chicken

    Working with one piece at a time, lift the chicken from the buttermilk and let excess liquid drip back into the bowl. Place it in the seasoned flour and press flour firmly over every surface and crease. Shake off loose excess flour and place the piece in a single layer on the clean tray. Keep wet and dry hands separate when possible, and discard the remaining flour and dip that have contacted raw chicken.

  5. Fry in Batches

    Carefully lower 3–4 pieces into the hot oil, depending on the pot size. Do not crowd the pot. The oil temperature will drop; adjust the burner to bring it back toward 325°F (163°C) and keep it near that temperature while the chicken cooks.

  6. Check Doneness by Temperature

    Fry until the crust is crisp and golden and each piece reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, with the thermometer away from bone. Smaller pieces may finish first; remove them individually when ready. Frying time varies with cut and size, so use temperature rather than a fixed number of minutes. See USDA poultry-temperature guidance.

  7. Drain and Repeat

    Transfer cooked chicken to the wire rack; do not stack or cover it, which can soften the crust. Let the oil return to 350°F before adding the next batch, then regulate it back toward 325°F as the chicken fries.

Phase 5

Serve

⏱️ Plate immediately after the chicken drains briefly

  1. Plate the Meal

    Spoon the red beans over the cooked rice and garnish with green onions. Serve with macaroni and cheese, warm honey-butter biscuits, and freshly fried chicken.

Cook's Notes

Timing, Temperature & Yield

  • The chicken pieces will not all cook at the same rate. Wings may finish before large thighs; check each piece separately.
  • Use the thermometer, not crust color alone. The coating can brown before a large bone-in piece is cooked through.
  • Keep the oil temperature controlled. Adding too many cold pieces at once can drop the oil temperature sharply and make the crust greasy.
  • The sides are abundant. This makes four generous plates, with possible leftover beans or mac and cheese.
