Cheesy Sausage Pinwheels

Cheesy Sausage Pinwheels — fast, crowd-pleasing party finger food requiring just 4 ingredients. Bake a batch for game day or gatherings and watch them vanish off the platter: flaky crescent dough rolled tight around browned savory sausage, cream cheese, and sharp Cheddar, sliced into swirls and baked golden.

Ingredients

Just 4 ingredients — recipe yields 2 logs.

🥐 Cheesy Sausage Pinwheels
Ground pork breakfast sausage500 g (~1 lb)
Refrigerated crescent roll dough2 cans (8 oz each)
Cream cheese, softened1 package (8 oz / 226 g)
Shredded Cheddar cheese1 cup

Tools needed: parchment paper, nonstick cooking spray, plastic wrap, and a sharp chef's knife.

Source: Insanely Good — Sausage Pinwheels (by Kim; updated September 12, 2024). Full structured translation of the original source.

Phase 1

Browning the Sausage

  1. Cook the ground sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat for about 10 minutes, breaking it up until evenly browned and crumbly. Drain off all excess rendered grease.

Phase 2

Prepping Dough, Layering & Rolling

  1. Preparing the Crescent Sheet

    Lay out a sheet of parchment paper and mist lightly with cooking spray. Unroll one can of crescent dough onto the parchment and firmly pinch all perforated seams together into a single uniform rectangle.

  2. Spreading the Filling

    Spread half of the softened cream cheese evenly across the dough, leaving a clean ½-inch (~1.5 cm) border along the edges. Scatter half of the cooked sausage and half of the shredded Cheddar evenly over the cream cheese.

  3. Rolling Tight

    Using the parchment paper to guide you, carefully roll the dough lengthwise into a tight, compact log. Wrap the finished log tightly in plastic wrap.

Tip Keep that ½-inch margin bare along the edges — that clean border prevents filling from squeezing out as you roll and seals the seam neatly for clean slices.
Phase 3

Second Log, Fridge Chill & Slicing

🧊 Minimum 1 hour chill — cold firm logs slice cleanly

  1. Repeat the exact process with the remaining ingredients to assemble the second log. Place both wrapped logs in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour until firm enough to slice cleanly.

  2. Slicing into Pinwheels

    Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Unwrap the chilled logs and use a sharp knife to slice crosswise into ½-inch (~1.5 cm) rounds.

Warning Do not shortcut the 1-hour chill! Warm, soft dough squashes under the blade and deforms the spirals; thoroughly chilled logs give crisp, round ½-inch pinwheels.
Phase 4

Baking & Serving

  1. Arrange the pinwheel rounds spaced apart on a baking sheet and bake for 10 to 15 minutes, or until puffed and golden brown.

  2. Remove the pinwheels from the oven and let cool slightly for a few minutes before serving warm.

Final Result Crisp golden pastry spirals packed with savory browned sausage and melted Cheddar-cream cheese — effortless party finger food guaranteed to disappear first.
