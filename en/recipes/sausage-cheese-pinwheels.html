Cheesy Sausage Pinwheels

Flaky, golden crowd-pleaser made with just 4 ingredients: browned savory sausage, tangy cream cheese, and melted cheddar rolled tight into crescent logs, chilled firm, sliced, and baked golden.

Ingredients

Makes 2 logs. Source: Insanely Good — Sausage Pinwheels.

4-Ingredient Pinwheels
Ground pork/beef sausage500 g (1 lb)
Refrigerated crescent roll dough2 cans (8 oz each)
Cream cheese, softened1 package (8 oz / 226 g)
Shredded cheddar cheese1 cup
Method

Roll, Chill & Bake

  1. Brown Sausage

    Cook ground sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat for ~10 minutes until browned and crumbly. Drain excess grease thoroughly.

  2. Seal Dough & Layer

    Lay out lightly greased parchment paper, unroll 1 crescent package, and pinch perforations into a seamless sheet. Spread half the softened cream cheese leaving a ½-inch (1.5 cm) clean border, then top with half the sausage and half the cheddar.

  3. Roll Tight & Chill 1+ Hour

    Use the parchment to roll lengthwise into a tight log, wrap tightly in plastic, repeat with the second batch, and refrigerate both logs at least 1 hour (up to 24 hours) so they slice cleanly without squishing.

  4. Slice & Bake

    Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Slice logs into ½-inch (1.5 cm) rounds with a sharp serrated knife, space onto baking sheets, and bake 10–15 minutes until golden brown. Cool briefly and serve warm.

Reference
