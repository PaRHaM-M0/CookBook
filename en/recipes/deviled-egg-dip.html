Deviled Egg Dip

Deviled Egg Dip — bold, velvety, tangy, and delicately spiced, guaranteed to become your new go-to party centerpiece. Imagine every bit of classic deviled-egg goodness transformed into a rich, scoopable dip right in the middle of the table — zero tedious piping into egg white halves required.

Ingredients

All quantities preserved verbatim from the original recipe.

Source: Insanely Good — Deviled Egg Dip (by Kim; published December 20, 2024). Full structured edition.

🥚 Deviled Egg Dip
Hard-boiled eggs, peeled12 large
Cream cheese, softened to room temp113 g (4 oz)
Mayonnaise⅓ cup
Dijon Mustard2 tbsp
Distilled white vinegar2 tbsp
Paprika (+ extra for garnish)½ tsp
Salt½ tsp
Black pepper¼ tsp
Hot sauce (Tabasco or Sriracha)A few dashes (optional)
Fresh chives, finely chopped1 tbsp
Phase 1

Boiling, Ice-Shocking & Peeling the Eggs

  1. Hard-Boiling the Eggs

    Arrange the eggs in a single layer in a large pot and cover with cold water until submerged by about 2 inches (~5 cm). Bring to a rolling boil, immediately turn off the burner, cover tightly with a lid, and let stand for 12 minutes.

  2. Ice Bath & Easy Peeling

    Transfer the cooked eggs straight into an ice-water bath to halt cooking completely and prevent gray-green yolk rings. Once chilled, peel under cool running water so shells slip right off.

Phase 2

Coarse Pulse First, Silky Whip Second

🥣 Pulse coarse first, then blend silky smooth at the end

  1. Quarter & Coarse-Pulse

    Quarter the peeled eggs and drop them into a food processor along with the softened cream cheese. Pulse several times until coarsely chopped.

  2. Emulsify Remaining Seasonings

    Add the mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, ½ teaspoon paprika, salt, and black pepper, pulsing to combine. Pour in the white vinegar and hot sauce (if using), then run continuously until smooth, velvety, and scoopable.

Texture Sequence Pulse just the eggs and cream cheese coarse first; full silky emulsification happens only at the final stage once vinegar and hot sauce go in. Avoid dumping everything in for one long spin from the start.
Phase 3

Garnishing, Chilling & Serving

🧊 Minimum 1 hour refrigerator chill for peak flavor & scoopable body

  1. Garnish

    Spoon the finished dip into a serving bowl and dust the top with extra paprika and chopped fresh chives.

  2. Chill & Serve

    Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving so flavors meld and the body firms up. Serve cold surrounded by crackers and crisp vegetables. Enjoy!

Appendix

Entertaining Guide, Dippers & Wine Pairings

🍽️ Cold party mezze / cocktail-hour centerpiece

  1. Best Role on the Table

    Built specifically as a crowd-friendly appetizer and cocktail snack: classic deviled eggs reimagined as a rich centerpiece dip where guests scoop freely with bread, chips, or vegetables.

  2. Does It Work with Tortilla Chips & Wine?

    Exceptionally well. Its savory-creamy body brightened with sharp Dijon mustard and white vinegar completely masks any eggy sulfur notes and makes an addictive bar snack alongside drinks:

    • Crunchy Dippers: Sturdy tortilla chips, salted butter crackers, toasted baguette crostini, crisp celery sticks, and sliced Persian cucumbers.
    • Crisp White Wines: High-acid dry whites such as chilled Sauvignon Blanc or Pinot Grigio slice cleanly across the mayonnaise and cream cheese richness.
    • Sparkling Wines: Eggs, butter/cream textures, and mustard chime effortlessly with dry bubbles like Prosecco or Cava — a classic sommelier pairing.

Source & Make-Ahead Notes

  1. Source: Insanely Good — Deviled Egg Dip by Kim (published December 20, 2024), preserving the full original headnote and tips.

  2. Storage & Pro Tips (From Source): Keeps up to 4 days tightly covered in the fridge — stir briskly before serving and wait until service time to add paprika and fresh chives. Always bring cream cheese fully to room temperature to prevent lumps, use high-quality mayonnaise, and don't skip those few drops of hot sauce even for mild crowds (Sriracha adds gentle sweetness; Tabasco adds bright vinegar snap).
