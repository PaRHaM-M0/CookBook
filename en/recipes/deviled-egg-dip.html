Deviled Egg Dip

All the rich, tangy, zippy magic of classic deviled eggs with zero piping hassle — whipped smooth in a food processor with cream cheese, Dijon mustard, white vinegar, and fresh chives.

Hard-Boiled Eggs
12 Eggs
Covered Steep
12 Mins Off Heat
Chill Before Serving
At Least 1 Hour
Fridge Storage
Up to 4 Days
Skill Level
Easy
1 · Boil & Ice Bath 2 · Process Smooth 3 · Chill & Pairings

Ingredients

Source: Insanely Good — Deviled Egg Dip.

Deviled Egg Dip
Hard-boiled eggs, peeled12 large
Cream cheese, softened to room temp113 g (4 oz)
Quality mayonnaise⅓ cup
Dijon mustard2 tbsp
Distilled white vinegar2 tbsp
Paprika (+ extra for garnish)½ tsp
Salt + Black pepper½ tsp + ¼ tsp
Hot sauce (Tabasco or Sriracha)A few dashes (opt.)
Fresh chives, finely chopped1 tbsp
Step 1

Foolproof Hard-Boiled Eggs

  1. Place eggs in a large pot covered by 2 inches (5 cm) cold water. Bring to a rolling boil, immediately turn off heat, cover tightly, and let stand 12 minutes.

  2. Plunge into an ice bath to halt cooking and peel smoothly under cool running water.

Step 2

Two-Stage Food Processor Emulsion

  1. Quarter peeled eggs and pulse in a food processor with softened 113 g cream cheese until coarsely chopped.

  2. Add mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, ½ tsp paprika, salt, and black pepper and pulse to combine; add white vinegar and hot sauce and process until velvety smooth.

Step 3

Chill, Garnish & Serving Pairings

  1. Transfer to a bowl, dust with paprika and chopped chives, and chill at least 1 hour before serving (keeps airtight up to 4 days).

  2. Best Dippers & Wine Pairings

    Serve with salted crackers, tortilla chips, toasted baguette rounds, celery sticks, and crisp cucumber. Pairs brilliantly with crisp high-acid dry whites (Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Grigio) or sparkling Prosecco / Cava. Source: Insanely Good — Deviled Egg Dip.
