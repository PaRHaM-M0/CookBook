Chicken Alfredo

Belly Full's Famous Chicken Alfredo — golden pan-seared herb chicken breasts over tender ribbons swathed in the dreamiest velvet cream sauce, ready in just over 30 minutes. Three standalone components combine into restaurant magic: crisp-seared juicy chicken, al dente fettuccine, and a lush garlic-Parmesan cream emulsion.

Ingredients

Serves 6. Metric equivalents: 16 oz = 454 g, 1 lb = 454 g, ½ cup butter = 113 g.

🍝 For the Pasta
Dried fettuccine pasta16 oz (454 g)
🍗 For the Golden Chicken
Boneless, skinless chicken breasts1 lb (454 g)
Italian seasoning1 tsp
Kosher Salt¾ tsp
Black pepper¼ tsp
Extra-virgin olive oil2 tbsp
Unsalted butter1 tbsp
🥛 For the Parmesan Cream Sauce
Butter, cut into large cubes or pats½ cup (113 g)
Heavy whipping cream2 cups
Fresh garlic, finely minced1 clove
Garlic powder¾ tsp
Italian seasoning¾ tsp
Salt¼ tsp
Black pepper¼ tsp
Freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano2 full cups

Source estimated nutrition per serving: 919 kcal, 45 g carbs, 39 g protein, 65 g fat, 1179 mg sodium.

Where this third Alfredo fits inside CookBook: Momsdish builds a clean 4-ingredient cream-butter base, Tasty thickens milk with flour, and Belly Full delivers the richest, cheesier classic — 2 full cups of freshly grated Parmesan, layered garlic + Italian seasoning in both chicken and sauce, and sliced golden chicken fanned on top.

Mise en Place First Like a stir-fry, this dish moves quickly once heat is on — measure, grate your cheese, and mince garlic before lighting the burner.
Phase 1

Boiling the Fettuccine

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the fettuccine to al dente per package directions (~10 minutes). Scoop out and reserve ½ cup starchy pasta water before draining.

Phase 2

Pan-Searing Golden Chicken

🍗 5–7 minutes per side undisturbed + 3-minute rest

  1. Season both sides of the chicken breasts evenly with Italian seasoning, ¾ tsp salt, and ¼ tsp black pepper.

  2. Heat olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering, swirling to coat. Lay chicken in and sear undisturbed for 5 to 7 minutes until deep golden-brown underneath.

  3. Flip, drop 1 tablespoon butter between the breasts, swirl gently as it melts, and cook another 5 to 7 minutes until an instant-read thermometer hits 165°F (74°C).

  4. Transfer to a cutting board, rest 3 minutes, slice crosswise into ½-inch strips, and tent loosely with foil while building the sauce.

Never Guess Chicken Doneness Use an instant-read thermometer and pull right at 165°F — carryover heat finishes gently while resting under foil.
Phase 3

Building Alfredo Sauce in the Same Pan

🥛 Gentle simmer (never a hard boil) — 3 to 4 minutes whisking constantly

  1. Drop burner heat under the same skillet to medium-low, add the ½ cup cubed butter and heavy cream, and whisk until melted together.

  2. Whisk in the minced garlic, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper until smooth.

  3. Bring to a gentle bubble (do not boil hard) and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, whisking constantly until slightly thickened.

  4. Whisk in the freshly grated Parmesan just until melted and velvety smooth — splashing in reserved starchy pasta water a couple tablespoons at a time if thicker than desired.

Skip Pre-Shredded Bagged Cheese Grate real wedge Parmigiano-Reggiano fresh yourself! Pre-grated tubs contain anti-caking cellulose that refuses to melt smoothly and turns cream sauces grainy.
Phase 4

Assembling & Serving

  1. Pull the sauce off the heat and immediately toss with the drained fettuccine.

  2. Divide coated ribbons into warm bowls, fan sliced golden chicken on top, and garnish with chopped fresh parsley, extra Parmesan, and cracked black pepper.

Pasta Shapes & Sides Flat wide fettuccine or linguine cling best, ridged penne traps sauce inside tubes, and homemade garlic bread, crisp green salad, cucumber-tomato salad, or roasted asparagus round out the table.
Finish

Troubleshooting, Storage & Source

  1. Too Thin vs. Too Thick

    Too thin? Simmer 1–2 minutes longer or pull off heat and let stand 1–2 minutes — it thickens fast as temperature drops. Too thick? Splash in warm reserved pasta water. Substituting half-and-half or whole milk lowers calories at the cost of a thinner coat.

  2. Proper Storage

    • Make-Ahead: Best built fresh right before eating, though chicken breasts can be seared up to 1 day ahead.
    • Leftovers: Refrigerate tightly sealed up to 3 days.
    • Reheating: Warm slowly in a skillet over low heat, stirring gently (high microwave heat splits butterfat out of emulsion).

  3. Source: Belly Full — Chicken Alfredo {Easy & Amazing!} by Amy Flanigan (updated February 21, 2025; rated 4.99/5 across 426 reviews). Complete unabridged edition.

Pantry Appendix

Reconstructing 2 Cups Heavy Cream from 30% Cream

  1. No 36% heavy whipping cream locally? Whisk 60 g warm melted unsalted butter slowly into 420 g 30% breakfast cream to yield ~480 g at ~36% milkfat (full derivation in our Chicken Mushroom Alfredo page).

Note Those 60 g butter belong strictly to the cream reconstruction — keep both the ½ cup sauce butter and 1 tbsp chicken butter intact.
