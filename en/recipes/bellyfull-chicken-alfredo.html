Chicken Alfredo (Belly Full)

Amy Flanigan's Showstopper Chicken Alfredo — golden pan-seared Italian-seasoned chicken breasts sliced over tender fettuccine in an ultra-velvety 2-cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cream sauce in 33 minutes.

Servings
6 People
Total Time
33 Mins (15 Prep + 18 Cook)
Rating
4.99 / 5 (426 Reviews)
Chicken Target
165°F (74°C)
Skill Level
Medium
1 · Fettuccine 2 · Golden Chicken 3 · 2-Cup Parmesan Sauce 4 · Toss, Slice & Storage

Ingredients

Serves 6. Source: Belly Full — Chicken Alfredo. Use freshly block-grated Parmigiano-Reggiano (never pre-shredded).

Noodles & Pan-Seared Chicken
Dry fettuccine pasta (reserve ½ cup cooking water!)16 oz (454 g)
Boneless skinless chicken breasts1 lb (454 g)
Italian Seasoning + Kosher salt + Black pepper1 tsp + ¾ tsp + ¼ tsp
Extra-virgin olive oil + Butter2 tbsp + 1 tbsp
 Velvety Parmesan Sauce
Butter, cubed + Heavy whipping cream½ cup (113 g) + 2 cups
Minced garlic clove + Garlic powder + Italian Seasoning1 clove + ¾ tsp + ¾ tsp
Salt + Black pepper¼ tsp + ¼ tsp
Freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano2 full cups
Step 1

Boil Fettuccine & Save Pasta Water

  1. Cook fettuccine in salted boiling water until al dente (~10 mins). Reserve ½ cup starchy pasta water before draining.

Step 2

Golden Butter-Swirled Chicken

  1. Season breasts with 1 tsp Italian seasoning, ¾ tsp salt, and ¼ tsp pepper. Sear undisturbed in shimmering olive oil over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes until deep golden; flip, drop 1 tbsp butter between pieces swirling gently, cook 5–7 minutes more to 165°F (74°C), rest 3 minutes under foil, and slice ½-inch thick.

Step 3

3–4 Minute Simmered Cream Sauce

  1. In the same unwashed pan over medium-low heat, melt ½ cup butter into 2 cups heavy cream (or our 420 g 30% cream + 60 g butter equivalent). Whisk in minced garlic, ¾ tsp garlic powder, ¾ tsp Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper; hold at a gentle simmer (do not boil!) whisking constantly for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened, then melt in 2 cups fresh Parmigiano-Reggiano smooth (thin with reserved pasta water if needed).

Step 4

Toss Immediately & Storage

  1. Toss hot fettuccine in sauce off heat immediately, plate topped with warm sliced chicken and fresh parsley. Keeps airtight up to 3 days in the fridge; reheat gently over low stovetop heat. Source: Amy Flanigan — Belly Full.
