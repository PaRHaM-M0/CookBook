72-Hour Poolish Pizza Dough

Phase Zero Master Dough — engineered specifically so 8–12 minute bakes in a 250°C home oven stay pillowy, open-crumbed, and explosive rather than drying out: an overnight honey-kissed Poolish paired with a strong flour blend at 71% hydration.

Yield
4 to 5 Dough Balls (~250 g each)
Total Hydration
71% (500 g Water / 700 g Flour)
Start Window
72 to 80 Hours Before Bake
Poolish Ferment
1h Counter + 16–24h Fridge
Skill Level
Advanced
Day 1 · Honey Poolish Day 2 · Final Dough & Slap-Fold Days 2–3 · Bulk Cold Ferment Fridge & Freezer Storage

Ingredients

Weigh strictly in grams. Yields 4–5 × ~250 g pizza balls.

Day 1 — Poolish Preferment
Room-temp water (~25°C)200 g
Fresh Yeast (or Instant/Active Dry Yeast)5 g fresh (or 2 g dry)
Honey5 g
Strong Bread Flour or Tipo 00200 g
 Day 2 — Final Dough
All of the ripe Day-1 PoolishFull batch
Cold water (chilled in warm weather)300 g
Flour blend: 300 g strong Manitoba/bread flour + 200 g Tipo 00500 g
Fine sea salt20 g
Extra-virgin olive oil (added last)10–15 g
Day 1

Building the Poolish (72–80h Before Bake)

  1. Whisk 200 g room-temp water, 5 g fresh yeast (or 2 g dry), and 5 g honey in a tall lidded container until dissolved, fork in 200 g flour until wet and lump-free, leave uncovered on the counter 1 hour to wake up, then seal airtight and refrigerate 16 to 24 hours until honeycomb-bubbly and fragrant.

Day 2

Final Mixing, Olive Oil Finish & Slap-and-Fold

  1. Let bubbly poolish temper 1 hour at room temp, scrape into a large mixing bowl, dissolve 20 g sea salt in 300 g cold water, and work in 500 g flour alternating with the salt water until all dry flour clears the bowl.

  2. Knead in 10–15 g extra-virgin olive oil last, turn out onto an bare counter, and work dynamically via Slap & Fold for 10–15 minutes with damp hands (never add extra flour!) until silky smooth and passing the translucent Windowpane Test.

Days 2–3

Bulk Rest & Cold Fermentation to Hour 72

  1. Rest covered 1 hour at room temp with stretch-and-folds, transfer bulk mass into a lightly oiled airtight wide container on the bottom shelf of the fridge, and cold-ferment until baking day — then ball, top, and bake following Phases 2–9 of our Neapolitan Pizza!

Storage

Extended Fridge & Freezer Protocol

  1. Short Delay in Fridge (+24h Max)

    Portion into lightly oiled 250 g balls sealed individually; use within at most 4 days total from Day 1 poolish start before gluten slackens.

  2. Freezing Individual Balls (Up to 3 Months)

    Freeze right after Day 2's 1-hour rest (never freeze raw unmixed poolish!): portion 250 g balls, flash-freeze firm on a tray, wrap skin-tight in plastic inside air-free freezer bags. Peak quality 1 month, ceiling 3 months (King Arthur · Serious Eats). Thaw wrapped in the fridge 12–24 hours, then proof covered at room temp 2–4 hours before stretching.
