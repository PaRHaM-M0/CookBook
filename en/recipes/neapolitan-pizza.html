Phase 1

Mixing & Strengthening the 72-Hour Dough

🕰️ Start strictly 72 hours before bake day

  1. AVPN Water-First Direct Mixing Sequence (Before Autolyse)

    Calibrated specifically to yield two ~270 g dough balls at 70% hydration, following the official water-first mixing sequence of the True Neapolitan Pizza Association (AVPN) — always begin with water in the bowl, never dry flour first:

    • 1. Dissolve Salt in Water: Place your mixer bowl on the digital scale, tare to zero, pour in 221 g cool water (16–22°C), add 9 g fine sea salt, and stir until 100% dissolved.
    • 2. Build the 10% Flour Buffer Slurry: Whisk in strictly 32 g Tipo 00 flour (~10% of total flour) until smooth into a pancake batter — shielding delicate yeast cells from harsh direct osmotic shock against concentrated salt water.
    • 3. Dissolve Micro-Dose Yeast: Crumble and dissolve 0.9 g fresh cake yeast (or 0.3 g instant dry yeast per AVPN's exact 1:3 dry-to-fresh conversion) directly into the slurry, moving promptly so raw salt-yeast contact never exceeds 5 minutes.
    • 4. Rain Remaining Flour Gradually: Engage your dough hook on low speed and rain in the remaining 283 g Tipo 00 flour across steady increments, letting each wave hydrate and scraping bowl walls clean (or work first with a wooden spoon then lightly wet hands).
    • 5. Stop Point Before Autolyse: Shut the mixer off as soon as all dry flour disappears into one unified, slightly shaggy, tacky mass — full silky strength comes effortlessly from Autolyse + Stretch & Folds rather than overheating dough under long mechanical kneading.
    Formula Math 315 g flour + 221 g water + 9 g salt + 0.9 g fresh yeast = ~546 g total dough → cleanly dividing into two 270 g Neapolitan balls. Authentic Neapolitan dough contains strictly flour, water, salt, and yeast — zero sugar and zero oil inside the dough.
    AVPN Method Citation Water → salt → 10% protective flour buffer → yeast → gradual remaining flour follows the official Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana (AVPN) International Regulations, with yeast micro-dosed specifically for a 72-hour cold ferment (do NOT use 0.3 g dry yeast for short same-day rises!).
    Poolish Preferment Alternative Prefer contemporary airy home-oven crust with honey & olive oil? Swap straight over to our standalone 4–5 pie 72-Hour Poolish Pizza Dough, then return right here to Phase 2 onward for sauces, toppings, and baking!

  2. 30-Minute Autolyse Rest

    Once all initial dough ingredients are mixed together into a shaggy mass, switch the mixer off. Cover the bowl tightly and let the dough rest undisturbed at room temperature for 30 full minutes. This quiet hydration phase allows the flour to absorb every drop of water and relaxes the nascent gluten network, making every subsequent step effortlessly smooth.

  3. Building Gluten via Stretch & Fold Sets

    Because your dough sits at a supple 70% hydration, it relies on gentle folding rather than aggressive kneading to build a strong gas-trapping gluten web. Perform 3 to 4 sets of Stretch & Folds spaced 30 to 45 minutes apart:

    • Technique: Lightly wet both hands with cool water so dough never sticks. Reach under one side of the bowl, stretch gently upward until you feel smooth elastic resistance without tearing,
    • Fold cleanly over across the center of the dough mass,
    • Quarter-turn the bowl 90° and repeat along the remaining 3 sides (4 folds total = 1 complete set),
    • Cover tightly and rest 30 to 45 minutes before the next set.
    Visual Cue After Set #3 your dough should already feel pillowy, smooth, taut, and dotted with early blisters (Set #4 is purely optional if still slack).

  4. 72-Hour Sealed Cold Fermentation

    Round the smooth bulk dough neatly inside a lightly oiled bowl, seal airtight under plastic wrap or snap lid, and slide immediately into the refrigerator for 72 hours — locking slow fermentation, zero sour harshness, and deep complex pizzeria wheat flavor.

Phase 2

Preparing the Pizza & Dipping Sauces Ahead

🕰️ 1 day before OR morning of bake day

Make all sauces ahead and chill in the refrigerator so bake-day assembly runs completely stress-free.

Golden Law of Pizza Sauce Less is always more! Drowning raw dough under heavy wet sauce is the #1 amateur mistake responsible for soggy, soupy center crusts.

Method 1: Fresh Blanched Plum Tomatoes (Pro Level)

  1. Select 400 g firm, meaty, deep-red Roma / San Marzano plum tomatoes.

  2. Score a shallow “X” into the bottom skin of each tomato with a paring knife.

  3. Plunge into rapidly boiling water for strictly 30 to 60 seconds just until skins curl back along the X.

  4. Lift immediately with a slotted spoon straight into an ice-water bath to halt cooking completely so fresh raw tomato flavor stays bright.

  5. Slip skins off by hand, halve tomatoes lengthwise, and scoop out and discard 100% of watery seeds and jelly pockets — weighing strictly 200 to 240 g pure dense flesh across both pizzas on your digital scale.

  6. Crush the clean tomato flesh thoroughly with clean bare hands — NEVER use a blender or food processor, which whips air in and liquefies cell walls watery!

  7. Season lightly with fine salt (just enough to wake natural sweetness without tasting salty) plus several hand-torn fresh sweet basil leaves.

  8. Restraint with Olive Oil Inside Sauce

    Cap raw olive oil in fresh sauce at no more than ½ teaspoon total (or skip completely) — excess raw olive oil turns grassy-bitter under fierce oven heat. Cover and refrigerate so basil perfumes the crushed pulp.

Method 2: High-Grade Tomato Paste Shortcut

  1. Spoon 2 heaping tablespoons rich double-concentrated tomato paste into a small bowl.

  2. Stir in 4 to 6 tablespoons cold water spoonful by spoonful until loosened to smooth stirred-yogurt spreadability (spreads effortlessly without running).

  3. Stir in a pinch of salt, hand-torn fresh basil leaves, and 1 spoonful extra-virgin olive oil; taste, adding at most a tiny two-finger pinch of sugar only if paste tastes overly sharp.

Signature Italian Herb Dipping Sauce (Optional Crust Dipper)

Spicy Fermented Sauce Option Craving complex funky heat instead? Swap straight to our 5-day Lacto-Fermented Spicy Tomato Sauce crafted specifically for high-heat pies!

  1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together 3 tablespoons mayonnaise (the creamy base), 1 tablespoon French dressing (for gentle sweetness and tang), and 1 teaspoon ketchup (for warm color and savory depth) until completely uniform.

  2. Rub 1 tsp dried thyme and ½ tsp dried oregano or dried basil briskly between your palms to crush and release essential oils straight into the bowl.

  3. Stir in 1 small finely crushed garlic clove (optional), freshly ground black pepper, and ½ tsp white vinegar or fresh lemon juice for bright contrast.

  4. Crucial Emulsion Step

    While whisking rapidly without stopping, stream in 1 tablespoon neutral sunflower or canola oil in a gossamer thread until thick, glossy, and restaurant-velvety. Cover and chill at least 4 to 5 hours (or overnight) so herbs and garlic fully bloom.

Phase 3

Precision Topping Mise en Place

📦 Pre-weighed station eliminates every split-second delay

To execute your pizza build at high speed without leaving the hot oven door standing open, stage every single topping below across your workstation ahead of time strictly from your checklist (Mise en Place):

Full Luxury Topping Encyclopedia Exploring smoked marinated turkey, crispy Guanciale, post-bake Burrata, Mortadella-pistachio, or Taleggio? Dive into our dedicated Pizza Toppings Reference!

  1. Dual Cheese Prep (Mozzarella + Danish Esrom)

    Across two pizzas, weigh out 60 to 80 g (just over two small packs) of Danish Esrom cheese — prized for its rich, pungent, creamy stretch — alongside 160 to 200 g base Mozzarella (or a Mozzarella-Cheddar blend). That locks in strictly 80–100 g Mozzarella and 30–40 g Esrom per individual pie.

    CRITICAL PREP & STORAGE RULE Because Esrom delivers concentrated “bursts of savory flavor,” dice it cleanly into uniform 1 cm small cubes so its rich fat and aroma distribute evenly across every slice. Weigh each pizza's exact cheese portions into separate freezer zip-top bags, press out 100% of trapped air before sealing (preventing dry, hardened cheese edges in the cold fridge air), and refrigerate until bake time.

  2. Firm White Mushrooms (Skull Cross-Section Cut)

    Buy snow-white, tightly closed firm button mushrooms the day of baking — or store unwashed inside a dry brown paper bag in the fridge (never plastic or pre-washed, which turns mushrooms slimy black!). Slice cleanly crosswise down through cap and stem in classic pizzeria “skull” silhouettes, strictly capping at 8 to 12 thin slices per pizza so released mushroom water never floods the center soggy.

  3. Salami / Pepperoni

    Count and lay out your sliced mushrooms alongside 10 to 15 slices Salami/Pepperoni per pie on dedicated plates.

    Artisan Pork Upgrade Swap standard salami anytime for cubed homemade Porchetta-Infused Bacon chilled firm from your charcuterie stash!

  4. Staging Parchment, Heavy Foil & Clear Runway

    Cut four square sheets of parchment paper slightly wider than your finished pizzas, plus two extra-heavy-duty aluminum foil squares measuring roughly 45 × 45 cm. Do one dry-run walk-through from your prep counter straight to the oven rack, confirming a flat, wide launch board is ready in hand.

Phase 4

De-Chilling, Scaling 270 g Balls & Final Ambient Proof

⏱️ Pull from fridge 2.5 to 3 hours before bake time

Seventy-two hours have arrived — showday begins!

  1. Pull your billowing, long-fermented dough mass from the refrigerator onto a lightly floured counter and scale portions precisely on your digital gram scale. A single authentic standard personal Neapolitan ball weighs roughly 270 g — stick closely to this weight so each finished pizza stretches cleanly to a 28 to 30 cm diameter.

  2. Freezing Extra Dough Balls Ahead

    Any extra scaled dough balls left over from today's batch store beautifully for up to 3 full months in the freezer — simply rub each ball with a light sheen of oil and seal inside an air-purged zip-top freezer bag!

  3. For tonight's bake, focus strictly on your two active weighed ~270 g portions, rounding each one gently into a taut, satin-smooth sphere.

  4. Set both dough balls well apart inside a lightly olive-oiled proofing tray, seal completely airtight under plastic wrap topped with a damp kitchen towel so zero dry skin forms across their domes, and leave untouched in a warm room for 2 to 3 full hours until completely acclimated.

    PROOF COMPLETION SIGNALS Look for full doubling in volume, airy cloud-like puffiness, pillow-soft touch, and complete return to room temperature right down to the center — cutting this rest short leaves tight, cold elastic cores that fight back and tear mid-stretch.
Phase 5

Charging Your Oven Thermal Battery & Line Check

🔥 Minimum 60 to 90 minutes uninterrupted preheat

Maximum conductive bottom heat + radiant top heat make or break Cornicione puff in a home kitchen:

  1. Position Thermal Mass High

    Place your pizza stone, inverted heavy cast-iron skillet, or thick steel sheet on the highest upper rack of your oven so radiant dome heat blasts the top while stored thermal mass sears the crust floor.

  2. Crank Absolute Max Temperature

    Run your oven flat-out at maximum temperature (typically 250°C / 500°F+) uninterrupted for a full 60 to 90 minutes — the thermostat chime after 15 minutes means air is hot while stone/iron is still lukewarm!

  3. Strict Closed-Door Discipline

    Never crack the oven door open once during preheating.

  4. Final Pre-Flight Line Checklist

    Once baking kicks off, every single second counts — you have zero time to hunt for mushrooms or trim parchment paper. Across this 60-minute preheat window, check off every single item below:

    • Pizza sauce uncovered with dedicated spoon ready.
    • Pre-weighed bowls/bags of Mozzarella and cubed Esrom open.
    • Counted Salami/Pepperoni and sliced mushrooms side-by-side.
    • Cut parchment squares staged flat.
    • Small bowl of 50/50 Tipo 00 flour + fine Semolina ready for dusting.
    • Zero obstacles between stretching board and hot oven door.
Phase 6

Hand-Stretching Pie #1 & Millimeter Topping Build

✋ Pure pizzeria craft — gentle hands preserve 72 hours of gas

Bake hour has arrived. Select your first fully puffed, room-temperature dough ball and bring it gently to the bench for stretching.

ABSOLUTE CARDINAL LAW NEVER touch a rolling pin to Neapolitan dough! A rolling pin crushes 72 hours of delicate fermentation gas flat in two seconds and bakes dense cracker biscuit.

A) Step-by-Step Hand Stretch

  1. Dust your work counter generously with a 50/50 blend of flour and fine Semolina. Lift one dough ball gently and press both top and bottom faces thoroughly into the flour-semolina bed so the surface is completely coated and never sticks to your hands.

  2. Piano-Key Degassing from Center Outward

    Lay the coated dough disc flat on the bench. Using the flat pads of both sets of fingertips — exactly like playing piano keys — press gently starting from dead center and working outward, herding all precious fermentation gas bubbles cleanly out toward the perimeter rim.

  3. Sacred Untouched Cornicione Border

    Leave a clean 1 to 2 cm outer perimeter border 100% untouched all 360° around the edge — never press it flat! That untouched gas-filled ring puffs dramatically inside the hot oven into your signature blistered Cornicione collar.

  4. Knuckle Drape & Gravity Stretch

    Now that you have a small flat disc ringed by a plump border, lift the dough gently off the bench, curl both hands into soft closed fists, and drape the dough across the backs of your knuckles just like holding a car steering wheel.

  5. Draw your fists gently apart while rotating the disc steadily hand-over-hand so tension distributes evenly all around — letting natural gravity pull the dough downward and outward cleanly without tearing.

  6. Continue rotating cleanly until the pizza reaches 28 to 30 cm across; the center floor should become paper-thin — translucent enough to pass faint light through when held up — while remaining completely intact.

    Instant Micro-Tear Patch Plucked a tiny thin hole? Zero panic — overlap the edges slightly and pinch firmly shut with your fingertips before moving to parchment.

B) Immediate Transfer onto Parchment Paper

Your single most important move before touching tomato sauce: lay the bare stretched dough immediately onto a square sheet of parchment paper! Lifting a paper-thin dough disc once wet and heavy with sauce and cheese is physically impossible — build 100% of your toppings right on top of the parchment.

C) Precision Gram-Balanced Topping Sequence

Balance Over Overload Restraint produces crisp centers and molten harmony — weigh your first few pies until eye memory locks in!

  1. 1. Translucent Sauce Spiral (60–80 g MAX)

    Total tomato sauce across the entire pizza must NOT exceed 60 to 80 g (strictly 2 to 3 tablespoons). Spoon sauce right into the dead center and spiral outward with the flat back of your spoon in one smooth circular motion until pale white dough still peeks faintly through a thin crimson veil — keeping your 1 to 2 cm outer Cornicione border 100% spotless and dry.

  2. 2. Base Mozzarella Scatter (80–100 g)

    Take 80 to 100 g Mozzarella and scatter it evenly and loosely across the sauced floor. Never pack a thick wall-to-wall carpet of raw cheese — leaving small open red windows between shreds is completely intentional because melting cheese flows outward to blanket the pie seamlessly.

  3. 3. Aromatic Esrom Flavor Bombs (30–40 g)

    Take 30 to 40 g of your 1 cm cubed Danish Esrom and dot the cubes randomly with generous spacing nestled among the Mozzarella shreds so every slice delivers savory pockets of melted flavor.

  4. 4. Spaced Salami / Pepperoni (10–15 Slices)

    Lay 10 to 15 slices of Salami/Pepperoni gently across the cheese layer. Slight edge overlap near the border is fine, though leaving a touch of breathing room lets high heat curl and crisp every slice rim beautifully.

  5. 5. Counted Skull Mushrooms (8–12 Slices MAX)

    Arrange strictly 8 to at most 12 thin skull-cut mushroom slices across the top. Never crowd mushrooms onto a Neapolitan pie — mushrooms release significant water under intense heat, and overloading them puddles liquid right in the center.

Phase 7

Sequential High-Heat Bake & 3-Second Pit-Stop Swap

⚡ Never bake two pies simultaneously in a home oven!

Pie #1 now sits clean, precisely engineered on its parchment launch sheet, primed and completely ready to plunge into the roaring heat of the oven.

Home systems lack commercial recovery capacity: baking two pies simultaneously collapses oven heat and steams crusts pale and doughy.

  1. Launch Pie #1

    Crack oven door quickly, slide parchment-mounted Pie #1 straight off your flat board onto the screaming-hot upper stone/cast-iron, slam door shut immediately, and set timer around ~12 minutes.

  2. Build Pie #2 During Pie #1's Bake Window

    Zero idle standing around! Across those ~12 minutes, hand-stretch Dough Ball #2 onto Parchment Sheet #2 and complete 100% of its exact sauce, Mozzarella, Esrom, Salami, and mushroom build on deck right beside the stove.

  3. Visual Doneness Check Through Glass

    Peek strictly through closed oven glass over final minutes: cheese must bubble furiously across center while Cornicione puffs golden-brown with dark blister spots.

  4. 3-Second Pit-Stop Swap Sequence

    Execute like a Formula 1 pit crew:

    • Swing oven door open,
    • Grip parchment corner of finished Pie #1 with long tongs/spatulas and slide cleanly out,
    • Land Pie #1 straight onto your staged Thermal Tent Station (or brief wire rack so bottom breathes crisp),
    • In that exact same motion, slide staged Pie #2 straight onto the blazing stone,
    • Slam oven door shut inside 3–4 seconds flat!
    Heat Retention Law Every extra open-door second dumps 15–25°C of radiant dome heat needed for Pie #2's spring.
Phase 8

Thermal Tent Packaging Engineering (Zero Soggy Crust)

📦 Engineered foil air-tent + peak steam vents

Wrapping hot pizza tight in plain foil traps steam into soggy rubber crust and glues molten cheese straight to metal. Build an elevated Parchment-Lined Foil Thermal Tent instead:

Stage a wide 45 × 45 cm heavy-duty foil sheet flat on your counter with a clean dry square of parchment paper centered on top as moisture barrier.

  1. Land Hot Pizza

    Set piping-hot Pie #1 onto the centered parchment sheet.

  2. Raise Foil Dome Walls

    Bring left and right long foil edges straight up high above the center of the pizza — never pressing foil down against melted cheese!

  3. Chip-Bag Crimp Seal

    Fold those meeting top edges neatly downward over themselves 2–3 times like sealing a bag of chips, suspending a roomy warm air dome well clear above toppings.

  4. Gift-Wrap End Locks

    Fold open front and back ends neatly into crisp triangles and tuck snugly underneath the base to lock lateral draft shut.

  5. Crucial 3-Hole Peak Steam Vent

    Immediately pierce strictly 3 or 4 small knife-tip vents right along the highest top ridge (never low on sides!) — letting excess moisture escape upward as vapor instead of condensing back down onto crust while trapping 95% of radiant heat inside.

Repeat Immediately on Pie #2 Run the exact same thermal-tent build the moment Pie #2 clears the oven.
Phase 9

VIP Car Passenger-Seat Transport Protocol

🚗 Delivering pizzeria-crisp 30–40 minutes down the road

Dropping hot foil tents straight onto a cold angled bucket car seat ruins everything: cold upholstery condenses steam wet underneath while seat bucket tilt slides molten cheese sideways! Build a flat thermal cradle in 60 seconds:

  1. 1. Pre-Warm Cabin Air

    Start your car and run cabin heat 5 minutes ahead of departure so boxes never hit cold air shock.

  2. 2. Dead-Level Rigid Base on Passenger Seat

    Slide front passenger seat back and wedge a wide cutting board, inverted flat baking sheet, or thick rigid appliance cardboard flat across the bucket seat cushion until 100% table-level.

  3. 3. Thick Bath-Towel Insulation Bed

    Drape a thick dry cotton bath towel (or fleece throw) across the leveled board leaving half overhanging long enough to fold completely over top.

  4. 4. Load Foil Tents Level

    Carry both vented foil tents flat from kitchen to passenger seat side-by-side.

  5. 5. Full Thermal Swaddle

    Fold the overhanging heavy bath towel snugly all over and around the tents — locking heat inside while cotton fibers wick away every trace of vented steam without condensation!

Mission Accomplished Follow every phase from Day 1's 72-hour dough through VIP swaddle and your pies still arrive 30 to 40 minutes away singing crisp underneath with molten, blistered pizzeria heat. Bravo & Buon Appetito!
