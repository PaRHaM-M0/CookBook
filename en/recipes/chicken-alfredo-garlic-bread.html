Chicken Alfredo Garlic Bread

A halved crusty Italian loaf slathered with fresh garlic-parsley compound butter, layered with scratch garlic-Parmesan cream sauce, smoked-paprika seared chicken slices, and bubbling golden mozzarella.

Oven Temp
350°F (175°C)
Chicken Sear
4–5 Mins/Side to 165°F
Oven Bake
20–25 Minutes
Skill Level
Medium
1 · Smoked-Paprika Chicken 2 · Alfredo Sauce 3 · Garlic Butter 4 · Stack & Bake

Ingredients

1 lb ≈ 454 g · ½ cup butter = 113 g. For enriching 1 cup light 30% cream: whisk 210 g cream + 30 g melted butter.

Spiced Chicken
Boneless skinless chicken breast, halved lengthwise & pounded even1 lb (454 g)
Smoked paprika + Garlic powder1 tbsp + 1 tbsp
Black pepper + Onion powder + Salt1 tsp + 1 tsp + to taste
Clarified butter or cooking oil2 tbsp
 Alfredo Cream Sauce
Butter + Finely minced fresh garlic2 tbsp + 2 tbsp
Unsweetened heavy whipping cream1 cup
Freshly grated Parmesan + Black pepper + Salt½ cup + 1 tsp + to taste
 Garlic Compound Butter & Assembly
Softened unsalted butter + Fresh garlic + Fresh parsley + Pinch salt½ cup + 1 tbsp + 1 tbsp
Crusty Italian bread loaf + Shredded Mozzarella1 loaf + 1 cup
Step 1

Sear the Spiced Chicken

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Pat pounded chicken dry, coat both sides with salt, black pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and onion powder, and sear in 2 tbsp clarified butter/oil over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side until internal temp hits 165°F (74°C). Rest briefly and slice.

Step 2

Simmer the Alfredo Sauce

  1. Melt 2 tbsp butter over medium-low heat, sauté 2 tbsp minced garlic until fragrant, pour in 1 cup heavy cream, bring just to a bubble, whisk in ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan gradually with 1 tsp black pepper and salt, and simmer gently until thickened.

Step 3

Mix Garlic-Parsley Butter

  1. Mash together ½ cup softened unsalted butter, 1 tbsp finely minced garlic, 1 tbsp chopped parsley, and a pinch of salt (cousin to our standalone Herb Compound Butter).

Step 4

Layer & Bake Until Golden

  1. Slice Italian loaf in half lengthwise, spread 2–3 tbsp garlic butter across cut faces, spoon over Alfredo sauce, scatter 1 cup mozzarella, shingle sliced chicken on top, drizzle more Alfredo and parsley, and bake 20–25 minutes at 350°F until edges are crisp and cheese bubbles golden. Serve hot with remaining warm Alfredo for dipping.
