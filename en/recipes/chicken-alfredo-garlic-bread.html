Chicken Alfredo Garlic Bread

Chicken Alfredo Garlic Bread — a whole split Italian loaf slathered with fresh garlic-parsley compound butter, layered with scratch garlic Parmesan Alfredo sauce, shredded Mozzarella, and smoky paprika-seared chicken strips, then baked until crisp-edged and bubbling golden. Serve hot with warm extra Alfredo for dipping.

Oven
350°F (175°C)
Chicken Sear
4–5 mins/side to 165°F (74°C)

Ingredients

Metric notes: 1 lb ≈ 454 g; ½ cup butter = 113 g.

🍗 For the Spiced Chicken
Boneless, skinless chicken breasts — sliced horizontally & pounded even1 lb (454 g)
SaltTo taste
Smoked paprika1 tbsp
Garlic powder1 tbsp
Black pepper1 tsp
Onion powder1 tsp
Clarified butter (ghee) or cooking oil2 tbsp
🥛 For the Scratch Alfredo Sauce
Fresh garlic, finely minced2 tbsp
Butter2 tbsp
Unsweetened heavy whipping cream1 cup
SaltTo taste
Black pepper1 tsp
Freshly grated Parmesan cheese½ cup
🧄 For the Garlic-Parsley Compound Butter
Unsalted butter, softened½ cup (113 g)
Fresh garlic, finely minced1 tbsp
Fresh parsley, finely chopped1 tbsp
Salt (mixed into butter)Pinch
🍞 For Assembly
Whole Italian bread loaf1 loaf
Shredded Mozzarella cheese1 cup
Fresh chopped parsley (garnish)For topping
Phase 1

Seasoning & Searing the Chicken

🌡️ Preheat oven right away to 350°F (175°C)

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).

  2. Pat pounded chicken cutlets dry with paper towels and season generously on both sides with salt, black pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and onion powder.

  3. Heat clarified butter or oil in a skillet over medium heat; once shimmering, sear the spiced chicken for 4 to 5 minutes per side until golden and 165°F (74°C) internal.

  4. Transfer out to a cutting board, slice cleanly into strips, and set aside.

Phase 2

Simmering Scratch Alfredo Sauce

  1. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a separate saucepan over medium-low heat, stir in 2 tablespoons minced garlic, and sauté gently just until fragrant.

  2. Pour in 1 cup heavy cream, bring to a gentle simmer, and gradually whisk in ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan along with salt and 1 tsp black pepper.

  3. Simmer gently over medium-low heat until velvety and thickened.

Phase 3

Mixing Garlic Compound Butter

  1. In a small bowl, mash together softened butter, 1 tablespoon minced garlic, 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley, and a pinch of salt until uniform.

Cross-Reference Same culinary family as our standalone Herb Compound Butter page — stripped down here to pure garlic and parsley tailored for crusty bread.
Phase 4

Assembling & Baking Until Golden

🔥 Bake 20 to 25 minutes until bread crisps & cheese bubbles golden

  1. Slice the Italian loaf open lengthwise and spread 2 to 3 generous tablespoons of garlic compound butter across the cut crumb.

  2. Spoon a layer of warm Alfredo sauce across the bread, scatter shredded Mozzarella over top, and arrange the sliced smoky chicken strips evenly.

  3. Drizzle more warm Alfredo over the chicken and finish with chopped parsley.

  4. Set on a baking sheet and bake for 20 to 25 minutes until edges turn crisp and cheese melts into golden bubbles.

  5. Slice hot and serve with any remaining warm Alfredo sauce on the side for dipping!

Pantry Appendix

Reconstructing 1 Cup Heavy Cream from 30% Cream

  1. For 1 cup (~240 g) heavy whipping cream, slowly whisk 30 g warm melted butter into 210 g 30% cream until smooth (~36% milkfat — half batch of our Fettuccine Alfredo appendix).

Note Those 30 g butter belong strictly to the cream — keep both the 2 tbsp sauce butter and ½ cup garlic-bread butter intact.
