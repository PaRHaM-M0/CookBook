Lemon Chicken Orzo Soup

Salt & Lavender's Most Beloved Soup (4.93★ from 831 Votes) — wholesome, bright, light yet deeply comforting one-pot chicken soup with tender rice-shaped Orzo pasta and fresh lemon.

Prep Time
10 Minutes
Cook Time
40 Mins (50 Mins Total)
Yield
6 Servings
Rating
4.93 / 5 (831 Votes)
Skill Level
Easy
1 · Sauté Aromatics & Flour 2 · Poach Chicken in Broth 3 · Simmer Orzo & Bright Lemon Make-Ahead, Storage & Source

Ingredients

Serves 6 (298 kcal · 29 g Protein · 26 g Carbs · 8 g Fat). Source: Salt & Lavender.

Aromatic Base
Celery, finely chopped + Medium carrots, peeled & finely chopped2 sticks + 2 medium
Medium onion, chopped (sweet Vidalia or yellow)½ medium
Butter + Olive oil1 tbsp + 1 tbsp
Garlic, minced + All-purpose flour3 cloves + 2 tbsp
 Simmer & Bright Finish
Chicken Broth + Italian Seasoning6 cups + ¼ tsp
Uncooked boneless skinless chicken breasts (or thighs)1.5 lbs (~680 g)
Uncooked dry Orzo pasta1 cup
Fresh lemon juice (+ extra to taste / zest) + Fresh chopped parsley1 tbsp + 1 tbsp
Salt & freshly ground black pepperTo taste
Step 1

Sauté Mirepoix, Garlic & Flour

  1. Sauté finely chopped celery, carrots, and onion in 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes. Stir in 3 minced garlic cloves for 30 seconds, then cook 2 tbsp flour for ~1 minute.

Step 2

15-Minute Gentle Chicken Poach

  1. Pour in 6 cups Chicken Broth stirring until flour dissolves smoothly, add ¼ tsp Italian Seasoning and whole raw chicken breasts, bring to a boil, cover with lid slightly cracked open, and simmer gently 15 minutes (check 165°F / 74°C internal temp).

Step 3

Uncovered Orzo Simmer & Fresh Lemon Finish

Stir Frequently Uncovered!Keep the lid OFF during the 10-minute orzo simmer and stir often so orzo never sticks to the bottom of the pot.

  1. Stir in 1 cup uncooked orzo and simmer uncovered ~10 minutes until al dente (if using pre-cooked/rotisserie chicken, add it right now with the orzo).

  2. Pull chicken out, dice bite-sized, return to pot with fresh lemon juice (squeeze a full lemon + Microplane zest if you love bright citrus!), fresh parsley, salt, and black pepper. Serve immediately alongside crusty Sourdough Bread.

Storage

Make-Ahead Rule, Freezing & Source

  1. Orzo drinks up broth as it sits! For meal prep or freezing, cook/freeze the soup base without orzo and add freshly boiled orzo per bowl when reheating. Keeps 3–4 days chilled (splash extra broth in when warming). Source: Natasha Bull — Salt & Lavender.
