Lemon Chicken Orzo Soup

Salt & Lavender's Lemon Chicken Orzo Soup — the single highest-rated comfort soup on the site at 4.93 stars across 831+ reviews! Light, wholesome, one-pot magic that elevates classic chicken noodle comfort with tender rice-shaped Orzo pasta and vibrant fresh lemon juice.

Ingredients

Serves 6 — everything cooks start-to-finish inside a single Dutch oven or soup pot.

Aromatic Base & Light Roux
Celery ribs, finely diced2 ribs
Medium carrots, peeled & finely diced2 whole
Medium yellow or sweet Vidalia onion, finely diced½ onion
Butter1 tbsp
Olive oil1 tbsp
Garlic, finely minced / pressed3 cloves
All-purpose flour (for gentle broth body)2 tbsp
Broth Poach & Bright Finish
Chicken broth (Chicken Broth)6 cups
Homemade Italian Seasoning¼ tsp
Raw boneless, skinless chicken breasts1.5 lbs (~680 g)
Uncooked dry Orzo pasta (rice-shaped pasta)1 cup
Freshly squeezed lemon juice1 tbsp (or more to taste)
Fresh chopped Italian parsley1 tbsp (or more to taste)
Salt & freshly ground black pepperTo taste

Step 1

Sautéing Mirepoix & Cooking Off Flour

Medium-high heat — roughly 7 to 8 minutes

  1. Softening Celery, Carrots & Onion

    Melt butter with olive oil in a Dutch oven or soup pot over medium-high heat and sauté finely diced celery, carrots, and onion for 5 to 7 minutes until softened.

  2. Garlic & Light Flour Thickener

    Stir in minced garlic for 30 seconds just until fragrant (never browned), then sprinkle in 2 tablespoons flour and stir continuously for 1 full minute to cook off raw flour taste and lend silky body to the broth.

Step 2

Whisking Broth & Gently Poaching Whole Breasts

15 minutes gentle simmer with lid slightly cracked

  1. Adding Broth, Herbs & Whole Chicken

    Stream in 6 cups chicken broth, whisking well so the flour dissolves completely with zero lumps. Add ¼ teaspoon Italian seasoning and submerge the whole raw chicken breasts; bring just to a boil.

  2. 15-Minute Lid-Cracked Simmer

    Set the lid slightly ajar, drop heat to low, and simmer gently for 15 minutes.

Instant-Read Thermometer Tip Chicken breasts hit safe, juicy perfection right at 165°F (74°C) and dry out fast past that mark — check thickest centers with an instant-read thermometer rather than guessing.
Step 3

Cooking Orzo Uncovered, Chopping Chicken & Lemon Finish

10 minutes uncovered + regular stirring off bottom

  1. Simmering Orzo Uncovered

    Remove the lid completely, stir in 1 cup dry orzo, and simmer gently uncovered for about 10 minutes until tender al dente.

  2. Shredding/Chopping Chicken & Brightening

    Lift cooked chicken breasts onto a board, chop into bite-sized pieces (or shred with two forks), and return to the pot along with fresh lemon juice, fresh chopped parsley, and salt & black pepper to taste. Serve immediately!

Prevent Orzo Sticking Warning Keep the lid OFF and stir frequently across all 10 minutes — dense rice-shaped orzo sinks straight to the hot pot floor and sticks stubbornly if left alone!
Dial Up the Lemon! Natasha notes she listed 1 tablespoon lemon juice conservatively for cautious palates, while she and most readers squeeze in a full whole lemon (2–3 tbsp) + fresh Microplane lemon zest plus lemon wedges at the table.
Variations

Substitutions & Make-Ahead Golden Rule

  1. Boneless Thighs or Leftover / Broth Chicken

    Boneless skinless chicken thighs swap in 1:1. Using cooked shreddings from your Homemade Chicken Broth or rotisserie chicken? Skip adding meat in Step 2 and stir it in alongside the orzo for just the final 10 minutes.

  2. Golden Make-Ahead Rule: Cook Orzo Separately!

    Orzo acts like a sponge overnight and drinks up all broth in the fridge! Whenever meal-prepping ahead, boil and oil the orzo separately and spoon portions into individual bowls right before ladling hot soup on top.

  3. Best Sides

    Serve steaming hot with thick toasted Sourdough, warm baguette crust, or a peppery arugula-Parmesan salad.

Storage & Source

Refrigerating, Freezing & Original Source

  1. Fridge Storage & Reheating

    Keeps tightly covered in the fridge for 3 to 4 days; reheat gently on the stovetop with an extra splash of chicken broth to loosen pasta absorption.

  2. Freezer Protocol

    Freezes cleanly up to 3 months WITHOUT the orzo — freeze the finished chicken-vegetable lemon broth base and cook fresh orzo upon thawing so pasta stays springy.
