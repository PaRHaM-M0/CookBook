Better Than Pizza Hut Stuffed Crust Pizza

Homemade chewy dough water-sealed around 7–8 mozzarella sticks per pie, topped with guanciale-infused herb tomato sauce, baked on a blazing 500°F stone, and brushed immediately with hot garlic-Parmesan butter.

Yield
2 Pizzas
Prep Time
30 Minutes
Total Proofing
1 Hour 20 Mins
Stone Bake
6–10 Mins at 500°F (260°C)
Skill Level
Advanced
Phase 1 · Dough Phase 2 · Stone Preheat & Portion Phase 3 · Guanciale Sauce & Garlic Butter Phase 4 · Stuffed Seal & Bake Video Reference

Ingredients

Makes 2 stuffed-crust pizzas. Source: YouTube Video Reference.

Pizza Dough
Warm water (~95°F / 35°C) + Instant yeast1⅓ cup (320 ml) + 2½ tsp (8 g)
All-purpose flour (+ extra for dusting)3½ cups (500 g)
Fine sea salt + Granulated sugar1½ tsp (6 g) + 1 tbsp (14 g)
Extra-virgin olive oil2½ tbsp (35 g)
 Rich Guanciale Sauce & Garlic Butter
Finely diced Guanciale or thick bacon + Olive oil4 oz (113 g) + ¼ cup (60 ml)
Thinly sliced garlic + Crushed tomatoes4 cloves + 1 can (14.5 oz)
Fresh thyme + Fresh sage tied with twine4 sprigs + 3 sprigs
Salted butter + Finely grated garlic + Grated Parmigiano⅓ cup (85 g) + 3 cloves + 1 tbsp
 Assembly
Mozzarella string cheese sticks (7–8 per pie)14–16 sticks
Shredded low-moisture Mozzarella + Parmigiano1 cup + ½ cup
Thick-cut Pepperoni + Cornmeal (for peel) + WaterAs needed
Phase 1

Prepare the Dough

  1. Bloom 8 g instant yeast in 320 ml warm water (95°F/35°C) for 5 minutes. Whisk 500 g flour, 6 g salt, and 14 g sugar in a stand mixer bowl; run dough hook on low with 35 g olive oil and stream in yeast water.

  2. Knead 2–3 minutes by machine, then 2–3 minutes by hand until smooth and elastic. Ball into a greased bowl under greased plastic and proof 1 hour until doubled.

Phase 2

500°F Stone Preheat & Portioning

  1. Preheat pizza stone on the middle rack at max heat (~500°F / 260°C). Punch down doubled dough, divide into 2 equal balls, cover loosely without touching tops, and rest 15–20 minutes.

Phase 3

Guanciale Tomato Sauce & Garlic Butter

  1. Render 113 g diced Guanciale/bacon 3 mins over medium heat; add 60 ml olive oil and toast 4 sliced garlic cloves lightly. Pour in crushed tomatoes with the thyme-sage bouquet garni, season, simmer 5–8 minutes, and discard herbs.

  2. Melt 85 g salted butter, remove from heat immediately, and whisk in 3 grated garlic cloves + 1 tbsp finely grated Parmigiano.

Phase 4

Water-Sealed Cheese Ring & Stone Bake

  1. Roll one ball ¼-inch thin, ring the outer edge with 7–8 mozzarella sticks end-to-end, brush a light ring of water just inside the sticks, fold dough tightly over the cheese, and pinch forcefully down onto the dampened ring for a zero-leak seal.

  2. Move onto a cornmeal-dusted peel, spread sauce lightly in center, top with shredded mozzarella + Parmigiano + thick pepperoni, launch onto the 500°F stone for 6–10 minutes, brush hot crust generously with Garlic-Parmesan Butter right out of the oven, slice hot, and repeat for Pie #2!
