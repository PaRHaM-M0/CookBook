Cherry Cheesecakes

Tender, juicy mini cheesecake baskets sculpted from sieved 9% farmer/cottage cheese dough, filled with whipped cream cheese custard, crowned with fresh pitted cherries, and dusted with powdered sugar.

Oven Temp
375°F (190°C)
Ball Weight
75–80 g Each
Dough Rest
30 Minutes
Bake Time
25–30 Mins (Do Not Overbake)
Skill Level
Medium
1 · Dough, Cherries & Filling 2 · Shape & Bake 3 · Cool & Dust

Ingredients

Pro tip: pass 9% fat cottage/farmer cheese through a fine sieve first for ultra-smooth dough.

Farmer-Cheese Basket Dough
Farmer / cottage cheese (9% fat ideal, sieved smooth)800 g
Large eggs2 eggs
Sugar + Semolina4 tbsp + 4 tbsp
All-purpose flour2 tbsp
Vanilla powder + Pinch of saltTo taste + 1 pinch
Fresh cherries, halved & pitted (+ light sugar dust opt.)400 g
 Cream Filling
Cream cheese200 g
Egg yolk + Sugar (+ optional lemon zest/juice)1 yolk + 40 g
Butter (generous mold greasing) + Powdered sugarAs needed
Step 1

Mix Basket Dough & Whipped Filling

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Mix sieved farmer cheese, 2 eggs, 4 tbsp semolina, 2 tbsp flour, 4 tbsp sugar, pinch of salt, and vanilla smooth; roll into 75–80 g balls and rest 30 minutes.

  2. Wash, halve, and pit 400 g cherries. Beat 200 g cream cheese with 40 g sugar, then beat in 1 egg yolk (plus optional lemon zest).

Step 2

Shape Baskets & Bake 25–30 Mins

  1. Generously butter muffin/tart cavities, press each ball into a neat basket cup using a glass base or fingers, spoon cream filling nearly to the rim, top with halved cherries, and bake 25–30 minutes (pull promptly so baskets stay tender and juicy).

Step 3

Cool Slightly & Dust

  1. Cool briefly in pan, unmold carefully, and dust with snowy powdered sugar.
