Cherry Cheesecakes

Mini Cherry Cheesecakes 🍒 — tender little baskets shaped from Farmer Cheese dough, filled with silky sweetened cream cheese, and crowned with juicy fresh cherries. Baked until lightly golden — never overbaked so they stay lush and moist — then finished with a snowing of powdered sugar. 🤌🏼❤️

Ingredients

Exact quantities preserved verbatim from the original formula.

🧀 Farmer Cheese Basket Dough
Farmer cheese (or strained 9% cottage cheese)800 g
Eggs2 whole
Sugar4 tbsp
Semolina4 tbsp
All-purpose flour2 tbsp
Vanilla powderTo taste
Salt1 pinch
Fresh cherries400 g
🍒 Cream Cheese Filling
Cream cheese200 g
Egg yolk1 yolk
Sugar40 g
Butter (for greasing pan)Generous amount
Phase 1

Preheating & Three Quick Preps

🌡️ Oven: 375°F (190°C) — Dough ball rest: 30 minutes

  1. Preheat the Oven

    Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).

  2. Mix & Portion the Cheese Dough

    Combine the farmer cheese, eggs, semolina, all-purpose flour, sugar, salt, and vanilla powder until smooth and uniform. Roll into portioned balls weighing 75 to 80 g each and let rest for 30 minutes.

  3. Prep the Fresh Cherries

    Wash the cherries, halve them, and remove the pits. Toss lightly with a sprinkle of sugar if desired (optional).

  4. Whip the Cream Cheese Filling

    Beat the cream cheese and sugar together with an electric mixer until smooth, then add the egg yolk and beat again until silky. Add a splash of fresh lemon juice or finely grated lemon zest if desired.

Phase 2

Shaping the Baskets & Baking

🔥 25 to 30 minutes — do not overbake so the baskets stay moist and tender

  1. Butter your baking sheet or muffin pan generously. Set one rested cheese dough ball into each spot.

  2. Press the center down using the flat bottom of a glass or your fingers to shape neat little tartlet baskets with raised rims.

  3. Spoon the cream cheese filling into each basket without overfilling to the rim, then arrange fresh cherry halves on top.

  4. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes. Pull them promptly without overbaking so the cheese pastry stays soft, tender, and juicy.

Phase 3

Cooling, Dusting & Tips

  1. Remove the mini cheesecakes from the oven and let cool slightly before lifting from the pan and dusting with powdered sugar.

Curd Texture Tips If substituting cottage cheese for farmer cheese, press it through a fine sieve first for a smooth, lump-free dough — and 9% milkfat cottage cheese gives the richest texture.
