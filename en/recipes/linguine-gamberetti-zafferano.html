Linguine Gamberetti, Zucchine e Zafferano

Linguine with Red Shrimp, Zucchini & Saffron — straight from Italy's GialloZafferano. Shrimp and zucchini are an unbeatable Mediterranean pairing, elevated here by a velvety, glossy sauce built without a single drop of cream thanks to an intensely savory homemade crustacean fumetto (shell stock). Sweet red shrimp meet tender zucchini and golden saffron in a luminous first course.

Ingredients

Serves 4. Note: Italian q.b. (quanto basta) = as needed / to taste.

🍝 Linguine with Shrimp, Zucchini & Saffron
Linguine320 g
Red shrimp (Gamberi rossi), whole shell-on300 g
Zucchini300 g
Saffron powder (2 × 0.15 g packets)0.3 g
Shallot (Scalogno)50 g
Fresh marjoram leaves (Maggiorana)To taste (q.b.)
Cold waterAs needed (q.b.)
Extra-virgin olive oilAs needed (q.b.)
Black pepper1 pinch
Fine salt1 pinch

Dietary tags from source: naturally lactose-free & Mediterranean diet. Never discard the shrimp heads and shells — they build the entire fumetto backbone.

Phase 1

Cleaning the Red Shrimp & Simmering the Fumetto

🦐 Fumetto simmer: 10 minutes once returned to a boil

  1. Start by cleaning the whole red shrimp: twist off the heads, peel the shells (carapaci), and pull out the dark intestinal vein; set the clean shrimp meat aside in a bowl. Keep all heads and shells — they are essential for the fumetto.

  2. Build the fumetto: pour a ribbon of olive oil into a medium-large pot and heat until shimmering hot, then add the shrimp heads and shells.

  3. Roast them hard over brisk heat, crushing the heads down thoroughly with a wooden spoon to press out all coral and juices. Deglaze (sfumare) with cold water to cover and simmer for 10 minutes measured from the moment it returns to a boil.

  4. Strain the finished fumetto through a fine sieve into a bowl or pitcher and set aside.

Phase 2

Knife Cuts & Zucchini-Shrimp Base

  1. Slice the zucchini crosswise into half-moons roughly 5 mm thick. Finely mince the shallot and cut the peeled shrimp meat into 0.5 cm pieces.

  2. Warm a drizzle of olive oil in a nonstick skillet, add the chopped shrimp, sear for just a few moments until barely kissed with color, then immediately lift them out and set aside.

  3. In the exact same skillet, add the minced shallot and zucchini half-moons. Once the zucchini are tender and lightly golden, return the shrimp to the pan and cut the heat immediately. Season lightly with salt and black pepper.

  4. Ladle one spoonful of warm fumetto into a small cup and dissolve the saffron completely inside it.

Flash-Sear the Shrimp Only At this stage the shrimp merely kiss the hot pan for color and come right off; their final cooking finishes gently alongside the pasta at the end. Leaving them in the pan now turns them rubbery.
Phase 3

Finishing Pasta in the Pan & Mantecatura

🍝 Pull pasta 2–3 minutes shy of al dente; finish cooking directly in the pan with saffron & fumetto

  1. Boil the linguine in well-salted water, draining it 2 to 3 minutes short of package time while still very firm. Transfer directly into the skillet with the zucchini-shrimp base.

  2. Finish cooking the pasta over active heat, ladling in the dissolved saffron and hot fumetto gradually a little at a time, letting the starch drink in each addition before adding more.

  3. Off the heat, perform the final mantecatura: toss vigorously with a thread of raw extra-virgin olive oil, freshly ground black pepper, and fresh marjoram leaves until glossy.

  4. Plate and serve your shrimp, zucchini, and saffron linguine immediately!

Creaminess Without Cream The velvety coat comes purely from starch released during the last 2–3 minutes of cooking emulsifying with reduced shellfish fumetto and olive oil — zero dairy involved.
Finish

Storage, Chef Tip & Source

  1. Storage: Best eaten straight from the pan; leftovers keep up to 1 day refrigerated in an airtight container.

  2. Freezing Fumetto: Leftover strained shrimp fumetto freezes cleanly provided your starting shrimp were fresh and never previously frozen.

  3. Bonus Use: Save extra fumetto to build a deeply flavorful seafood risotto (risotto alla pescatora)!

  4. Source: GialloZafferano — Linguine gamberetti, zucchine e zafferano (rated 4.3/5; lactose-free, Mediterranean diet). Complete unabridged translation.
