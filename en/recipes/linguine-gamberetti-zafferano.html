Linguine with Shrimp, Zucchini & Saffron

GialloZafferano's Mediterranean Classic — creamy, golden, and velvety with zero heavy cream, powered entirely by a 10-minute roasted red-prawn shell fumetto, sweet zucchini half-moons, saffron, and fresh marjoram.

Servings
4 People
Prep Time
15 Minutes
Cook Time
20 Minutes
Skill Level
Easy
1 · Prawn Shell Fumetto 2 · Prawns & Zucchini 3 · Saffron Finish & Mantecatura

Ingredients

Serves 4 (Lactose-Free). Source: GialloZafferano.

Linguine, Prawns & Saffron
Linguine pasta320 g
Whole red prawns (Gamberi rossi — heads & shells reserved!)300 g
Zucchini, sliced into 5 mm half-moons300 g
Saffron powder (2 × 0.15 g sachets)0.3 g
Shallot, finely minced50 g
Fresh marjoram leaves, extra-virgin olive oil, cold water, salt & black pepperq.b.
Step 1

Roast & Simmer the Prawn Fumetto

  1. Twist off prawn heads, peel shells, devein tails, and dice meat into 5 mm pieces. In a hot oiled saucepan, roast all heads and shells vigorously, crushing them with a wooden spoon to release coral juices; deglaze with cold water, simmer 10 minutes from boiling, and strain clean (freezes well if made from fresh never-frozen prawns).

Step 2

Flash-Sear Prawns & Sauté Zucchini

  1. Flash-sear diced prawns in olive oil for just a few instants and remove immediately so they stay tender. In the same pan, sauté minced shallot and 5 mm zucchini half-moons until golden; return prawns off heat and season. Dissolve 0.3 g saffron in a ladleful of warm fumetto.

Step 3

Risottare Finish & Mantecatura

  1. Boil linguine in salted water 2–3 minutes shy of done, transfer to the zucchini pan, and finish cooking over heat while ladling in saffron and fumetto gradually until absorbed into a silky glaze. Off heat, toss briskly (mantecatura) with raw olive oil, black pepper, and fresh marjoram leaves. Serve immediately. Source: GialloZafferano.
