Chicken Mushroom Fettuccine Alfredo

Chicken Mushroom Fettuccine Alfredo — Momsdish's spot-on Olive Garden copycat: deeply savory, velvety, and loaded with tender golden chicken strips and seared mushrooms using just 10 everyday ingredients. Mushrooms are completely optional if someone at the table skips fungi, though their earthy umami elevates the cream sauce.

Ingredients

US measurements shown alongside exact metric weights: 1 lb ≈ 454 g, 2 cups heavy cream ≈ 473 ml, ½ cup butter = 113 g.

🍝 Chicken Mushroom Fettuccine Alfredo
Fettuccine pasta1 lb (454 g)
Fresh mushrooms1 lb (454 g)
Boneless skinless chicken breasts1 lb (454 g)
Heavy whipping cream2 cups (473 ml)
Unsalted butter½ cup (113 g)
Grated Parmesan cheese½ cup
Garlic, pressed2 cloves
Olive oil, for searing1 tsp
Salt (adjust to taste)1 tsp
Black pepper (adjust to taste)½ tsp

Source: Momsdish — Chicken Mushroom Fettuccine Alfredo (Olive Garden Copycat). Cooking where 36% heavy whipping cream isn't sold? Jump down to our dairy-fat math appendix below.

Why Fettuccine instead of spaghetti? Flat, wide egg ribbons give thick cream sauces maximum surface area to cling to, whereas round spaghetti suits lighter tomato sauces. Pull your pasta al dente (2–3 minutes shy of full softness) so it finishes absorbing hot sauce without going mushy.

Phase 1

Boiling the Fettuccine

  1. Cook the fettuccine in salted boiling water according to package directions until al dente, then drain.

Al Dente Rule True al dente retains a firm toothsome core (~2–3 minutes under full soft time) because the ribbons keep cooking gently once tossed into hot Alfredo sauce.
Phase 2

High-Heat Mushroom & Chicken Sear

  1. Slice the mushrooms thinly and sear over high heat until moisture evaporates and edges turn deep golden brown; season lightly with salt.

  2. Slice the chicken breasts into even strips matching the mushrooms, season with salt and pepper, and sear in olive oil until golden outside and cooked through.

Mushroom Flexibility Mushrooms are optional — omit freely if preferred, or mix and match cremini (baby bella), portobello, button, or wild chanterelles.
Phase 3

Velvety Garlic-Parmesan Alfredo Sauce

🥛 Medium-low heat — whisk gently just until smooth, melted, and unified

  1. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the heavy whipping cream, unsalted butter, pressed garlic, and grated Parmesan. Warm gently over medium-low heat, whisking until smooth, glossy, and unified.

Flavor & Thickness Troubleshooting Want punchier flavor? Add an extra pressed garlic clove, salt, black pepper, or a pinch of crushed red pepper flakes. Need a thicker coat? Whisk in extra grated Parmesan, a splash of starchy pasta water, or a tiny cornstarch slurry — keeping in mind the sauce naturally thickens further as it simmers and cools.
Phase 4

Tossing & Serving Hot

  1. Combine the drained fettuccine, golden mushrooms, and chicken strips directly in the warm sauce, tossing thoroughly until every ribbon is coated, then remove from heat.

  2. Serve immediately while hot topped with extra freshly grated Parmesan. Enjoy!

Pro Serving & Leftover Chicken Tips Use kitchen tongs to lift and coat every strand cleanly. Optional add-ins that pair beautifully: baby spinach, steamed broccoli florets, roasted asparagus, sweet peas, or chopped parsley/basil. Serve alongside warm garlic focaccia and crisp Caesar salad — and leftover cooked or rotisserie chicken works great warmed straight in the skillet.
Pantry Appendix

Converting 30% Breakfast Cream into 36% Heavy Cream

🇮🇷 Exact butterfat math when 36% Heavy Whipping Cream isn't available locally — without altering core recipe ratios.

  1. Understanding the Butterfat Gap

    Standard Persian/Middle-Eastern breakfast cream (such as Kalleh) contains 30% milkfat, whereas US Heavy Whipping Cream requires at least 36% milkfat. Bridging that 6-point fat gap simply takes unsalted butter, which carries 80–82% butterfat.

  2. Per 200 g Pack Formula

    Whisk 25 to 27 g melted butter (~2 tbsp) into every 200 g of 30% cream: 200 g cream supplies 60 g fat + 26 g butter supplies ~21.3 g fat = 81.3 g fat across 226 g total weight (~36% milkfat).

  3. Exact Batch Formula for This Recipe (2 Cups Heavy Cream)

    This recipe calls for 2 cups (473 ml / ~480 g) heavy cream. Whisk together 420 g 30% cream + 60 g warm melted butter to yield 480 g at ~36.5% milkfat — a 1:1 drop-in replacement.

  4. How to Emulsify Without Splitting

    Melt the 60 g butter and let it cool until merely warm (never piping hot), then stream it slowly into the cream while whisking constantly until homogeneous so no yellow butter pools on top. Use immediately just like standard heavy cream.

Two Crucial Warnings First: those 60 g of butter only bring the cream up to 36% — you still need the full ½ cup (113 g) sauce butter in the ingredient table (60 g + 113 g = 173 g butter total). Second: reconstructed cream excels in cooked sauces like Alfredo, NOT whipped pastry peaks. If using 35% cream, 4–5 g butter per 200 g (or using straight as-is) is already plenty.
Finish

Refrigerator Storage & Reheating

  1. Storage: Keeps fresh up to 1 week tightly sealed in the refrigerator without absorbing fridge odors. Chilled Parmesan firms the sauce up cold; warm gently over low heat with a splash of heavy cream (or milk) to restore silky flow.

Takeaway Homemade Alfredo is unapologetically rich yet completely free of the stabilizers and gums found in jarred grocery sauces — pure cream, real butter, pressed garlic, and aged Parmesan.
