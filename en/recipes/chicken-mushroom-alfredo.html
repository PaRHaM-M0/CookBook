Chicken Mushroom Fettuccine Alfredo

Momsdish's Olive Garden Copycat — flat egg fettuccine tossed with golden sautéed chicken strips and mushrooms in a silky 4-ingredient garlic-Parmesan cream sauce.

Ingredients

Source: Momsdish — Chicken Mushroom Fettuccine Alfredo.

Chicken Mushroom Alfredo
Fettuccine pasta1 lb (454 g)
Fresh mushrooms, thinly sliced1 lb (454 g)
Boneless skinless chicken breast1 lb (454 g)
Heavy whipping cream (36%+)2 cups (473 ml)
Unsalted butter½ cup (113 g)
Grated Parmesan cheese½ cup
Garlic cloves, pressed2 cloves
Olive oil (for frying)1 tsp
Salt + Black pepper1 tsp + ½ tsp (to taste)
Step 1

Boil Fettuccine Al Dente

  1. Boil flat fettuccine in salted water 2–3 minutes shy of full softness (al dente) so it finishes absorbing sauce without turning mushy; drain.

Step 2

Sauté Mushrooms & Chicken

  1. Sauté sliced mushrooms over high heat until deeply golden brown; season lightly with salt.

  2. Slice chicken breasts into even strips, season with salt and pepper, and sauté until golden and cooked through.

Step 3

5-Minute Garlic-Parmesan Cream Sauce

  1. In a medium saucepan over medium-low heat, whisk 2 cups heavy cream, ½ cup butter, 2 pressed garlic cloves, and ½ cup grated Parmesan until melted, smooth, and gently thickened.

Step 4

Toss with Tongs & Serve

  1. Add cooked fettuccine, golden chicken, and mushrooms into the sauce, toss thoroughly with tongs off heat, and serve warm topped with extra Parmesan. Stores airtight in the fridge up to 1 week (rehydrate with a splash of cream when warming).

Culinary Appendix

Enriching 30% Cream into 36% Heavy Cream

  1. Exact Dairy Fat Math

    Standard breakfast/table cream carries 30% fat, whereas US Heavy Whipping Cream carries at least 36% fat, and butter is ~80–82% fat. Whisking warm melted butter slowly into 30% cream bridges that 6-point fat gap seamlessly for cooked sauces:

    • Per 200 g pack of 30% cream: Whisk in 25–27 g (~2 tbsp) warm melted butter → yields ~36% fat cream.
    • Exact batch for this recipe's 2 cups (~480 g): Whisk 60 g warm melted butter slowly into 420 g of 30% cream → yields 480 g at ~36.5% fat!
ImportantThat 60 g butter enriches the cream itself and is in addition to the ½ cup (113 g) butter listed for the sauce!
