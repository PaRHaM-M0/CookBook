Detroit-Style Pan Pizza

The Definitive Architectural & Chemical Manual — not merely a recipe, but a strict 11-phase protocol pairing a 73% hydration bread-flour matrix with wall-planted Mozzarella & Fontina Frico edges, natural-casing pepperoni cups, hand-crushed racing stripes, ice-cold Ricotta, torn live basil, olive oil, and Hot Honey.

Phase 1

Dough Matrix Activation (Acoustic Kneading)

  1. Finger-whisk 1–1.5 tsp instant yeast into 300 g bread flour in the mixer bowl. Dissolve 9 g fine salt into 220 g body-temp water (98–105°F / 36–40°C) and pour into the flour.

  2. Mix slowly with the dough hook, scraping walls clean, then raise speed slightly until the dough audibly slaps and slams rhythmically against the bowl walls into a smooth cohesive mass.

Phase 2

Heavy Oil Pool & First Ambient Rise (2–3 Hours)

  1. Pool extra-virgin olive oil generously across the floor of your Detroit pan (this literally fries the bottom crust). Oil your palms thoroughly, transfer dough into the pan, gently coax toward corners without stabbing, seal top tightly with cling film, and let rest undisturbed 2.0 to 3.0 hours in a warm spot.

Phase 3

Hand-Crushed Sauce & Tin Restoration

  1. Dump the 28 oz whole peeled tomatoes into a heavy glass bowl and crush every bulb exclusively by clean hand for irregular chunky texture (never blend). Season aggressively with dried oregano, dried basil (never fresh pre-bake!), red pepper flakes, garlic powder, salt, and cracked pepper; whisk uniform, then spoon right back into its empty tin while waiting.

Phase 4

Precision Cheese Cubing

  1. Cube low-moisture Mozzarella on the left side of a plate; slice away the waxy rind of Fontina and cut into noticeably smaller dense cubes on the right side.

Phase 5

Hand-Sliced Natural-Casing Pepperoni

  1. Slice a whole natural-casing pepperoni stick into sturdy medium-thick discs (never paper-thin pre-sliced packs) so edges curl into crispy grease-cupping chalices.

Phase 6

Overnight Cold Fermentation (12–24 Hours)

  1. Once the dough jiggles like aerated jelly with visible surface bubbles after its 3-hour rise, transfer the sealed pan straight into the refrigerator for an overnight cycle (12–24 hours).

Phase 7

Pan Geometry & Mandatory Frico Wall Planting

  1. Pull cold pan from fridge, scatter Mozzarella cubes loosely across the center, then physically mash cold cubes of both Mozzarella and Fontina flush against every millimeter of the metal pan walls — this builds the iconic caramelized lacy Frico crown! Nestle pepperoni discs evenly across gaps including corners.

Phase 8

Three Racing Stripes & Microplaned Edge Crown

  1. Never coat the whole surface in sauce! Ladle three longitudinal racing stripes (center first, right flank second, left flank third), tuck tiny spoonfuls along the perimeter walls, and Microplane hard grating cheese aggressively all around the outer border.

Phase 9

500°F+ High-Heat Bake

  1. Bake dead center in an oven preheated to at least 500°F until perimeter cheese crowns turn deeply dark mahogany/jet-black crispy lace and top cheese blisters violently.

Phase 10

Arctic-to-Molten Finishers (Seconds Out of the Oven)

  1. Immediately drop spoonfuls of ice-cold Ricotta across molten bubbling zones, scatter freshly torn live basil, streak lightly with raw extra-virgin olive oil, and drizzle amber Hot Honey across crispy peaks.

Phase 11

Perimeter Release & Grid Decimation

  1. Run a flat steel spatula around all 4 pan walls to release the lacy crust onto a board — the fried underside should ring golden and crisp. Saw cleanly into thick vertical squares with a long serrated knife and devour.
