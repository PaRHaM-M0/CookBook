Cheesy Potato “Thing”

Weird “Cheesy Potato Thing” — “Would you try this weird cheesy potato thing? Mine came out like this 🥔” This page features slightly modified specs: stuffed inside with Mozzarella and Chorizo, and cooked in the Air Fryer instead of deep-frying. Mashed potato gets wrapped right around the cheese and Chorizo and turns golden-crisp at 240°C — eat it with a knife and fork 🤣

Ingredients

Makes 2 pieces (2 servings).

🥔 Cheesy Potato Thing
Peeled potatoes600 g
Melted butter2 tbsp
Flour5 tbsp
Mozzarella60 g
Chorizo30 g
Olive oil5 ml

Modifications in this version compared to the original recipe: added Mozzarella and Chorizo inside, and cooked in an Air Fryer instead of deep-frying.

Method

Shaping & Cooking; 8 Steps

🍟 Air Fryer at 240°C — 7 to 8 minutes per side, flipping halfway with a spatula

  1. Peel and boil the potatoes until tender, then drain well and mash.

  2. Add the melted butter and flour, mixing first with a fork and then with your hands until smooth; shape into one large ball with your hands.

  3. Lay out a sheet of cling film and drizzle the olive oil over it.

  4. Place the potato ball onto the oiled cling film and press it down into a large flat circle.

  5. Place the Mozzarella and Chorizo right in the center of the circle.

  6. Using the cling film to help lift, bring the potato up from all 4 sides and fold it over; press firmly to close and seal every seam completely.

  7. Cook in the Air Fryer at 240°C for 7 to 8 minutes per side, flipping halfway through with a spatula.

  8. Once cooked through and golden, eating it with a knife and fork is highly recommended 🤣

Key Shaping Tip The cling film serves both as your pressing mat and your 4-way folding tool. Drizzling olive oil onto the film keeps the potato dough from sticking, and sealing every gap shut in Step 6 is what locks the molten cheese inside while cooking.
