Cheesy Potato “Thing”

A viral mashed-potato dough parcel upgraded with smoky Chorizo and stretchy Mozzarella, sealed tight using cling film and air-fried at 240°C until blistered and golden — knife and fork strongly advised!

Yield
2 Servings
Air Fryer
240°C · 7–8 Mins per Side
Nutrition / Serving
480 kcal · 20 g Protein · 64 g Carbs · 16 g Fat
Skill Level
Easy
8-Step Method

Ingredients

Makes 2 servings. Original inspiration: @foodiefrontier0 (air-fryer & chorizo upgrade).

Potato, Mozzarella & Chorizo Parcel
Peeled potatoes600 g
Melted butter2 tbsp
All-purpose flour5 tbsp
Mozzarella cheese60 g
Chorizo30 g
Olive oil5 ml
Method

8-Step Build & Air-Fry

  1. Peel and boil 600 g potatoes until tender, drain thoroughly, and mash smooth.

  2. Work in 2 tbsp melted butter and 5 tbsp flour first with a fork, then knead smooth by hand into a single large ball.

  3. Lay out a sheet of cling film and drizzle 5 ml olive oil across the center.

  4. Press the potato ball onto the oiled cling film into a wide flat circle, place 60 g mozzarella and 30 g chorizo dead center, and use the cling film to fold all 4 sides cleanly over the filling, pressing and sealing every seam airtight.

  5. Air-fry at 240°C for 7–8 minutes per side, flipping carefully halfway with a wide spatula. Slice open hot with a knife and fork!
