Sourdough Bread

Complete artisan loaf blueprint from Phase -10 to Phase 100: scale-only precision, zero oil or sugar, just 1 g yeast, stretch-and-folds, 24–72 hour cold fermentation, and blistering Dutch-oven steam.

Yield
1 Artisan Boule
Hydration
75% (300 g Water / 400 g Flour)
Salt
8 g (2%)
Instant Yeast
Only 1 g (¼ tsp)

24 to 72 Hours

Ingredients

For 1 artisan loaf. Discard volume cups — use a digital scale only.

Fine sea salt (non-iodized preferred)8 g (2%)
Instant Yeast1 g exact (~¼ tsp)
Rules

Essential Principles Before Mixing

  1. Why Zero Oil or Sugar?

    Oil softens the crust and destroys the crackling blistered bakery shell. Pure flour, water, salt, and 1 g yeast deliver glass-crisp crust and complex fermentation aromatics.

  2. Freezing Advice

    Never freeze raw dough (ice crystals weaken gluten and yeast). Slice the baked, cooled loaf and freeze slices — straight from freezer to toaster tastes freshly baked.

Phase -10

Equipment Setup

  1. Large mixing bowl, precision digital gram scale, high-heat parchment paper, razor blade/lame, and a heavy lidded Dutch oven rated for 250°C (500°F).

Phase 1

Initial Mix & Autolyse (30 Mins)

  1. Scale 300 g water into the bowl, stir in 1 g instant yeast, add 400 g bread flour, and mix strictly by hand until zero dry flour remains (no kneading — a shaggy sticky mass is right). Cover and rest 30 minutes.

Phase 2

Salt Incorporation (30 Mins)

  1. Sprinkle 8 g fine sea salt over top, wet your hand, dimple and squeeze salt thoroughly into the dough, cover, and rest 30 minutes.

Phase 3

Stretch & Fold Strengthening

  1. Perform 3 sets of 4-sided stretch-and-folds with wet hands spaced 30 minutes apart (1.5 hours total). After the 3rd set, cover and let bulk rest untouched at room temp for 1 to 2 hours until ~30% puffed.

Phase 4

Shaping & Surface Tension

  1. Turn gently onto a barely dusted counter without popping bubbles. Stretch lightly into a square, fold all 4 corners to the center, flip seam-down, and cup-drag circularly toward yourself with both hands to build tight surface tension.

Phase 5

24–72 Hour Cold Retard

  1. Line a round bowl or Banneton with a lint-free cloth generously dusted with bread + rice flour. Place boule seam-side UP (smooth side down), fold cloth over, seal inside an inflated plastic bag, and refrigerate 24 to 72 hours.

Phase 6

Post-Fridge Bench Time: ZERO Minutes!

Pro SecretNever warm cold dough on the counter! Cold firm dough holds height, scores cleanly with a razor, and explodes with maximum Oven Spring when hit with 250°C steam.
Phase 7

Dutch Oven Steam Bake

  1. Preheat empty Dutch oven + lid at 250°C (500°F) for 1 full hour. Invert cold dough onto parchment, brush excess flour even, and slash a 1 cm deep curved score at a 45° angle.

  2. Lower parchment sling into the blazing Dutch oven, lid immediately, and bake 25–30 minutes at 250°C covered; remove lid, drop oven to 220°C (425°F), and bake 15–20 minutes uncovered until deep mahogany caramelized.

Phase 100

Mandatory 2-Hour Cooling Rest

Do Not Slice Hot!Cool on a wire rack untouched for at least 2 full hours while the 100°C crumb finishes setting. Slicing hot vents internal steam and turns the crumb gummy.
