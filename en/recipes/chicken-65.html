Chicken 65

India's legendary street-food flavor bomb: bite-sized boneless chicken thighs marinated in yogurt, Kashmiri chili, and fresh curry leaves, coated right in the bowl with cornstarch and rice flour, fried crisp, and flashed in a blistering Tadka of curry leaves and green chilies.

Golden RuleNever confuse Curry Leaves with yellow curry powder — they share zero flavor chemistry. Curry leaves are an aromatic citrus-pine leaf, and the final sizzling Tadka is where the magic happens.
Step 1

Power Spiced Yogurt Marinade (4 Hours)

  1. Combine bite-sized chicken thighs in a glass bowl with yogurt, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri chili powder, cumin, Garam Masala, salt, black pepper, and 1 torn handful of curry leaves. Massage thoroughly, cover, and refrigerate for 4 hours.

Step 2

In-Bowl Wet Coating (No Dry Dredge)

  1. Pull the bowl from the fridge and sprinkle ~2 tbsp cornstarch and ~2 tbsp rice flour directly into the marinated chicken. Mix by hand until a clingy, spiced paste-like batter coats every piece.

Step 3

Deep Frying

  1. Heat deep oil until hot. Drop battered pieces in one by one so they never clump, frying until deep reddish-gold and crisp with dark flecks of curry leaf. Drain and hold ready right beside your wok.

Step 4

The Magic Step: Sizzling Wok Tadka

  1. Heat 2–3 tbsp oil in a clean wok until shimmering hot. Immediately drop in sliced green chilies, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, ½ tsp chili powder, and 1 generous handful of fresh curry leaves — they will crackle violently and turn translucent-crisp in seconds.

  2. Without hesitation, dump all the hot fried chicken straight into the spiced oil and toss rapidly for just a few seconds until every piece glistens.

Step 5

Serve

  1. Pile onto a serving platter, squeeze fresh lemon juice generously over top, and eat hot.

Appendix

Pantry Substitutions & Scratch Prep

  1. Scratch Ginger-Garlic Paste

    Grate or pound equal parts peeled garlic and fresh ginger (e.g., 3 cloves garlic + 3 cm ginger knob) into a smooth paste.

  2. Dried Curry Leaves Trick

    Buy whole dried curry leaves from a spice shop (never curry powder!). Crumble dry into the yogurt marinade; for the hot Tadka, soak dried leaves in warm water for 10 seconds and pat completely dry on paper towel first so they bloom instead of scorching instantly.

  3. Kashmiri Chili & Quick Garam Masala

    Sub Kashmiri chili with 1 tbsp sweet paprika (not smoked) + a tiny pinch of hot cayenne. For scratch Garam Masala, combine equal parts ground cinnamon, cloves, green cardamom, cumin, and coriander plus a tiny pinch of nutmeg.
