Chicken 65

Chicken 65 — ranked #4 overall in the global fried-chicken showdown video and crowned by the judges as the single punchiest Flavor Bomb on the entire planet at ~9.7 / 10. Its magic hinges on two non-negotiable signatures: fresh curry leaves and the final seconds-long Tadka flash in spiced oil. Unlike Western or Korean fried chicken, there is zero dry dredge — starches fold directly into the spiced yogurt marinade to form a clinging batter paste.

What floored the judges on first bite was sheer flavor density: shatteringly crisp rice-flour crust injected right to the core with savory, tangy, herbaceous South Indian aromatics. Structurally, it flips our Korean Fried Chicken completely on its head — instead of dry potato starch + sweet sticky sauce, Chicken 65 uses a wet spiced paste batter finished in blistering aromatic chili-curry oil.

Golden Rule Use real curry leaves! Host Max emphasizes repeatedly how intoxicating fresh curry leaves smell both inside the marinade and — most critically — crackling inside the hot Tadka pan.
Step 1

Building the High-Aroma Indian Yogurt Marinade

🧊 Refrigerator marination rest: 4 hours

  1. Place the bite-sized boneless chicken thigh pieces into a clean glass mixing bowl.

  2. Add the thick yogurt, Ginger-Garlic Paste, Kashmiri Chili Powder, ground cumin, Garam Masala, salt, and freshly ground black pepper directly over the chicken.

  3. Tear or roughly slice the first handful of fresh curry leaves to bruise their essential oils and scatter them in.

  4. Massage thoroughly by hand until every piece is deeply coated. In chef Max's words from the video: “Hands down the best-smelling marinade on this entire list!”

  5. Cover tightly and refrigerate for 4 hours.

Why Kashmiri Chili Powder? Kashmiri chili delivers Chicken 65's signature deep crimson-red hue with mild, fruity warmth rather than blinding capsaicin burn — letting you use a full 1 to 2 tablespoons without wrecking your palate.
Step 2

Forming the Clinging Wet Starch Paste

🥣 Right inside the marinade bowl after 4 hours — zero separate flour station

  1. Pull the marinated chicken bowl out after its 4-hour chill — no separate dry dredging trays needed.

  2. Sprinkle the cornstarch and rice flour directly into the marinated chicken bowl.

  3. Work together thoroughly by hand until yogurt, spices, and starches fuse into a thick, sticky, paste-like coating hugging every piece of chicken.

Why Rice Flour + Cornstarch? Rice flour fries up glass-crisp without absorbing heavy grease, while cornstarch binds the yogurt spices tightly onto the meat so nothing sloughs off in hot oil.
Step 3

Frying Until Deep Crimson-Gold & Crisp

🔥 Fully hot oil; drop pieces singly so they never clump together

  1. Heat generous frying oil in a deep pot or wok until shimmering hot.

  2. Drop coated chicken pieces in one piece at a time so paste-coated bites never fuse into clusters.

  3. Fry until deep crimson-tinted golden brown and thoroughly crisp, flecked with blistered dark green curry leaves.

  4. Lift out onto a wire rack and keep right beside the stove for immediate Tadka tossing.

Step 4

The Magic Finale: Sizzling Curry-Leaf Tadka

✨ The true secret of Chicken 65 — where “all the magic happens” in just a few seconds

  1. Set a clean wide skillet or wok over high heat and add 2 to 3 tablespoons oil.

  2. The instant the oil shimmers hot, toss in the sliced green chilies, 1 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, ½ tsp red chili powder, and a big generous handful of fresh curry leaves.

  3. Stir immediately for just seconds — the curry leaves crackle and crisp dramatically as volatile oils infuse the hot fat.

  4. Without hesitating a beat, dump all the hot crispy fried chicken straight into the sizzling pan!

  5. Toss vigorously for just a few seconds so spiced crimson oil, crispy curry leaves, and charred green chili rings coat every cranny without softening the crust.

Split-Second Timing Warning Tadka is flash-infusion, NOT slow cooking! Pausing before tossing in the fried chicken scorches the garlic-chili oil bitter — keep your fried chicken waiting right next to the burner before starting.
Step 5

Plating & Fresh Lemon Finish

  1. Pile the glistening, crackling pieces onto a warm platter (served on black slate in the reference video) as curry-leaf aroma fills the room.

  2. Squeeze fresh lemon juice generously right over the top and eat immediately!

Final Result Glass-crisp exterior glossed in spiced chili-curry oil with shattering fried curry leaves — salty, fiery, citrusy, and deeply herbal all at once (~9.7/10).
Appendix

Pantry Sourcing & Substitution Guide (Hard-to-Find Items)

🇮🇷 Standalone pantry guide preserving 100% of original recipe specs when cooking outside South Asian grocery hubs (such as Iran).
✅ Easy Everyday Staples Chicken thighs, strained yogurt, rice flour, cornstarch, ground cumin, fresh hot green chilies, black pepper, cooking oil, and fresh lemons are everywhere — only 4 specialty items below need smart solutions.

  1. 1) Ginger-Garlic Paste — Make It Fresh in 60 Seconds

    • Scratch Formula: Skip jarred pastes completely! Peel equal weights of fresh garlic and fresh ginger root (e.g., 3 large garlic cloves + a 3 cm knob of ginger) and pound in a mortar or grate on a fine Microplane into a smooth paste — exactly how Indian home kitchens make it daily.
    • One batch covers both the 1 tbsp marinade dose and the 1 tsp Tadka dose.

  2. 2) Fresh Curry Leaves — Essential Strategy

    CRITICAL WARNING NEVER substitute yellow “curry powder” for curry leaves! Yellow curry powder is a dry spice blend and tastes completely unrelated to real Murraya koenigii curry leaves.
    • Best Substitute When Fresh Isn't Available: Source whole dried curry leaves from spice merchants / Indian grocers (resemble petite bay leaves).
    • In the Marinade: Crush dried curry leaves finely between your palms directly into the yogurt.
    • In the Hot Tadka: Steep whole dried leaves for just 10 seconds in warm water and pat dry on paper towels right before hitting hot oil so they rehydrate slightly and bloom aroma without scorching black instantly.

  3. 3) Kashmiri Chili Powder — Color Without Blistering Heat

    Used specifically for vibrant ruby-red color paired with gentle, approachable warmth.

    • Exact Scratch Match: Mix 1 tablespoon sweet Hungarian/Spanish paprika (strictly sweet, NOT smoked!) + a pinch to ¼ tsp hot cayenne / red chili powder calibrated to your heat tolerance.

  4. 4) Garam Masala — Store-Bought or 30-Second Blend

    • Availability: Widely stocked at spice shops; freshly ground at home smells twice as vibrant.
    • Quick Scratch Blend: Combine equal parts ground cinnamon, cloves, green cardamom, cumin, and coriander seed plus a tiny pinch of grated nutmeg.
Quick Action Summary Secure fresh or dried curry leaves first (the irreplaceable soul of Chicken 65), grate fresh 1:1 ginger-garlic paste, blend sweet paprika + hot chili for crimson color, and fry until crackling crisp!
