Harira Soup

Fragrant Moroccan tomato soup simmered with lamb, beef, or chicken, peeled chickpeas, lentils, warm ginger, cinnamon, abundant fresh herbs, and finished silky-smooth with traditional Tedouira.

Meat
225 g Lamb, Beef, or Chicken
Ahead Prep
Soak Chickpeas Overnight
Pressure Base
25 Mins Medium Pressure
Stovetop Base
50–60 Mins Gentle Simmer
Skill Level
Medium
1 · Prep 2 · Base Soup 3 · Option A: Rice 4 · Option B: Broken Vermicelli 5 · Tedouira & Serve

Ingredients

Includes both pressure-cooker and conventional pot timings.

Soup Base
Lamb, beef, or chicken, diced small225 g
Vegetable or olive oil3 tbsp
Soup bonesA few (optional)
Ripe soft tomatoes (~6 large)~900 g
Dried chickpeas, soaked overnight & skinned1 handful
Dried green or brown lentils, picked over2 handfuls
Large onion, grated into pulp1 large
Celery stalk with leaves, finely chopped1 stalk
Flat-leaf parsley & fresh cilantro, finely chopped1 small bunch each
Smen / clarified butter1 tbsp (optional)
 Spices
Salt + Ground ginger1 tbsp each
Black pepper1.5 tsp
Ground cinnamon (optional) + Turmeric1 tsp + ½ tsp
 Finishing & Tedouira Liaison
Tomato paste dissolved in 1–2 cups water3 tbsp
Raw rice OR broken fine vermicelli (pick one)3 tbsp
All-purpose flour whisked smooth with 2 cups water1 cup flour
Lemon wedges & fresh cilantroFor serving
Step 1

Advance Prep

  1. Skin the Chickpeas

    Soak dried chickpeas overnight, drain, and slip off skins between thumb and forefinger (or rub vigorously inside a kitchen towel).

  2. Tomatoes, Onion & Herbs

    Cook tomatoes briefly and pass through a food mill/strainer to discard skins and seeds. Grate or process onion to a thick paste. Finely chop celery, parsley, and cilantro.

Step 2

Simmering the Meat & Chickpea Base

  1. In a 6 L+ pressure cooker (or heavy pot), brown diced meat in oil over medium heat. Add bones, skinned chickpeas, tomato purée, grated onion, all spices, smen (if using), and 3 cups (710 ml) water. Bring to a boil and cook at medium pressure for 25 minutes (or simmer gently covered for 50–60 minutes).

  2. Stir in picked lentils, diluted tomato paste, chopped celery, parsley, cilantro, and 8.5 cups (2 L) water. Bring back to a boil, then follow either Step 3 OR Step 4.

Step 3

Route A — Finishing with Rice

  1. Cook at medium pressure for 30 minutes (or simmer gently 60 minutes), then stir in 3 tbsp raw rice and cook under pressure 15 minutes longer (or simmer 30 minutes).

Step 4

Route B — Finishing with Broken Vermicelli

  1. Instead of Step 3, cook at medium pressure for 45 minutes (or simmer gently 90 minutes), then stir in 3 tbsp broken vermicelli and simmer uncovered a few minutes until tender.

Step 5

Silky Tedouira Thickening & Serve

  1. Adjust seasoning, then slowly stream in the smooth flour-and-water slurry (Tedouira) while stirring constantly until creamy and velvety. Simmer 5–10 minutes, skimming any surface foam, and serve hot with lemon wedges.
