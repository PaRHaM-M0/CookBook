Tunacado Sandwich

The iconic Joe & The Juice sandwich engineered down to the millimeter: warm, butter-crisped artisan sourdough shattering against chilled, silky tuna salad, vibrant walnut-basil pesto, and fanned avocado — scheduled so dinner service takes under 10 minutes.

Section 1

Precision Shopping Guide

  1. Bread

    Skip thin, squishy supermarket sandwich bread. Buy bakery Sourdough (or bake our Sourdough Bread), Rustik, or thick rye sliced 1–1.5 cm thick.

  2. Tuna

    Buy 2 × 180 g cans of solid/chunk light fillet (never shredded/flaked mush). Once pressed bone-dry, two cans yield exactly 260 g pure meat (130 g per person).

  3. Produce & Pantry

    Vibrant green basil with zero black leaves (buy day-of), 2 fresh lemons (never bottled juice), slender young scallions, walnuts, Parmesan, Dijon mustard, 5 single-serve mayo packets, butter, and corn oil.

Section 2

Mastering Avocado Ripeness

  1. Timing & Paper-Bag Ethylene Trick

    Rock-hard avocados take 4–5 days on the counter to ripen. Need it faster? Seal the hard avocado in a brown paper bag with a banana or apple to ripen in ~2 days. Once ripe, move immediately to the refrigerator ("pause button") until dinner.

  2. Two Foolproof Physical Ripeness Tests

    • Tip-of-the-Nose Test: Press the tip of your nose — firm cartilage with a gentle 2–3 mm springy give. Forehead = unripe wood; soft cheek = overripe guacamole.
    • Thumb-Pad Test: Relax your palm flat and press the fleshy mound beneath your thumb — full, springy, sliceable perfection. Also flick off the stem cap: bright green underneath = pristine inside; dark brown = oxidized.

Section 4

Night-Before Prep (Zero Aroma Loss)

  1. Sterilize & Press Tuna Bone-Dry While Warm

    Boil unopened cans for 20 minutes, let cool until safe to handle while oil is still liquid (cold fridge oil solidifies and clings to meat!), open, and press the lid down with full body weight over the sink until every last drop drains. Flake in an airtight container and chill completely plain — zero lemon or pepper tonight (overnight lemon acid turns cooked tuna chalky!).

  2. Toast Walnuts

    Toast 30 g walnuts in a dry pan over medium-low heat for 3–4 minutes, shaking every 15 seconds until fragrant. Transfer immediately to a cool plate so residual heat doesn't burn them; store airtight at room temperature (fridge moisture ruins crunch).

  3. Separate Herb Storage

    Wash and thoroughly dry basil leaves; store in the fridge lined with dry paper towel far away from garlic and scallions. Store trimmed, unwashed, uncut scallions with peeled garlic in a separate sealed bag (chopping scallions the night before releases sulfurous stale odors!).

Section 6

Emerald Walnut Pesto (Mini-Chopper Method)

  1. Use a compact mini-chopper (never a tall smoothie pitcher where 30 g of leaves spin in empty air, or pound classically in a mortar). Pulse 1 garlic clove + 30 g cooled toasted walnuts 3–4 times until sandy.

  2. Add 30 g dry basil leaves, 2 tbsp Parmesan, ¼ tsp salt, ⅛ tsp black pepper, 1.5–2 tbsp corn oil, 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, and 1 small ice cube (or 1 tbsp ice water) — the ice keeps friction heat from blackening the chlorophyll! Pulse just until thick and spreadable like Greek yogurt.

Section 7

Tuna Salad & Ruler-Exact Scallion Cut

  1. Wash and dry 2 slender scallions, trim root hairs, measure 0 to 13 cm from the white base upward (2 cm white bulb + 11 cm crisp green tube; discard limp top tips), and slice into sub-1 mm rings yielding exactly 1.5–2 tbsp.

  2. Fork together cold dry tuna (260 g), minced scallions, 4 mayo packets, 1 heaped tsp Dijon mustard, juice of ½ fresh lemon, and a pinch of black pepper until creamy and cohesive (add the 5th mayo packet only if still dry).

Section 8

10-Minute Showtime Assembly

  1. One-Sided Butter Toast

    Melt 10 g butter per sandwich over medium heat. Lay bread slices down and toast ONLY ONE SIDE for 2–3 minutes until deep golden and shattering-crisp. Place toasted side DOWN on the board (crispy side faces outward; soft interior faces inward!).

  2. Fan the Avocado

    Halve, pit, scoop out whole halves, slice thinly on the board, and press gently into an angled green ribbon fan.

  3. Layer Bottom-to-Top

    Spread a generous layer of pesto on the soft inner crumb → lay the avocado fan edge-to-edge over the pesto → spoon a thick mound of cold tuna salad over the avocado → finish with flaky salt & cracked pepper → crown with the second slice of bread (crispy buttered face UP).

Section 9

Slice & Serve

  1. Hold firmly and saw cleanly in half with a sharp serrated bread knife for that signature café crunch. Serve immediately while the exterior is hot and the center is chilled.
